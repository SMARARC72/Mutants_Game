# MUTANTS_GAME — NPCs & World Reactivity (v0.1)

**Status:** roundtable-locked (orchestrated) · **Last updated:** 2026-06-27
**Principle:** *the world keeps a ledger on you, and it is not always flattering.*

---

## NPC types

- **Faction figures** — the 9 factions' leaders, quest-givers, and Hands.
- **Rivals** — the nemesis system (§Bosses): persistent, evolving, sticky.
- **Services** — vendors, breeders, lab-techs, trainers, healers.
- **Mortals** — the ordinary, who react to your rise with awe, fear, or worship.
- **Gods** — the Pantheon, as bosses *and* potential patrons.

## Dialogue tone

Funny-grim and characterful — **every NPC has an opinion and a body count of their own.** Register shifts by region/faction: gallows wit (the Pale Court), absurdist (the Revel/Sunder), deadpan occult bureaucracy (the Concord), warm-but-doomed (the Bloomwardens).

## World reactivity (the "decisions impact the world" pillar)

Your **morality grid + notoriety + faction standing + Succession acts** continuously reshape:

- **NPC attitudes** — awe, fear, hostility, reverence, recruitment.
- **Available content** — quests, regions, gear, and creatures gate open/shut by who you've become.
- **Region states** — kill a region's god and its NPCs, economy, and spawns change; corrupt a region and it rots; purify it and it blooms.
- **Faction power** — aligning hard tips the balance; clan wars resolve based partly on you.
- **Memory** — deeds are logged and referenced; the Graveyard and the Succession persist across runs.

## Reactivity cadence

**Gated/threshold** (matching the notoriety model): crossing a deed/grid/standing threshold triggers a discrete world shift — a faction turns, a region destabilizes, a god marks you, NPCs rewrite their lines. Not constant churn; punctuated, legible consequences.

## Ties
Grid & notoriety → Character ladder. Region shifts → §World/§Overworld. Factions → §Factions. Rivals → §Bosses. Cross-run memory → the Succession.
