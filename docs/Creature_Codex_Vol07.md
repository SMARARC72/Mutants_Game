**Scope:** batch4-001…batch4-050 (50 entries). The "light side" expansion — radiant holy (Eros/Cosmos) opening into a short dark interlude (Thanatos/Chaos), then frost/sky spirits (Ouranos/Cosmos), and the first two apex dragons.
**Status:** Vol7 complete — batch4-001…batch4-050 done.
**Method:** funny-grim · stats copied verbatim from the dossier (live `stat_engine.py`) · art read from `montage_batch4_p1.png` (b4-001…024), `p2.png` (b4-025…048), `p3.png` (b4-049…050), upscaled per-row for legibility.

> **Coordination flags for the systems session (do not silently fix — your call):**
> 1. **Cluster mislabel (cosmetic):** the build task framed b4-025…030 as "radiant/transition," but the dossier AND the art agree these are **dark** Thanatos/Chaos assassins (purple/black/red winged beasts and dragons). Entries follow the dossier. The radiant block is b4-001…024 only.

> 3. **Families are authored, not registry:** every `line_registry` is `[none]` and every name was authored. Lineages below (Gilt Hart, Sunfleece, Aureole, Seraphwing, Reliquary, Duskwidow, the Hoarfrost Court, Empyrean Flight) are **proposed clades grouped by body+force+art**, not hard registry chains. Ratify or override. All 50 are T2 Wild except the two Legendary dragons, so these read as **sibling mid-forms within a clade**, not baby→apex ladders.
> 4. **Dragon block continues elsewhere:** b4-049/050 are the first two of the dragon flight that runs on into b4-051+ (next volume's range). Treated as the radiant/azure head of that flight (Empyrean Flight); the darker, armored wyrms downstream are a separate sub-flight.
