# MUTANTS_GAME — Story Spine (v0.1)

**Status:** roundtable-locked (orchestrated) · **Last updated:** 2026-06-27
**Principle:** *a nobody learns the gods were all nobodies once — and decides to be the last one.*

---

## The four acts

**Act 0 — The Nobody.** You start in **Threshold** with the old knack. Tutorial: catch your first creature, run the Lab, feel the world (gods dead, thrones empty, force everywhere). **Inciting incident:** you do something only a god-aspirant could — survive a god's mark, or your starter *awakens* in a way that shouldn't happen. The **High Table** notices and "invites" you to play the game.

**Act 1 — The Climb.** Travel the regions, build your team and Lab, court factions, win competitions, hunt **Legendaries.** Rank **Mortal → Demigod.** Mid-act gut-punch: you learn the **Succession truth** — every god in the sky was once a mortal who climbed this exact ladder. The pantheon is a graveyard of winners.

**Act 2 — The Reckoning.** The pantheon notices you back. Gods and factions move to stop you; alliances harden along your **morality grid.** You begin **killing Olympians and taking their domains** — and each kill *reshapes the region* (its force runs wild, its faction reels). Rank **Demigod → Titan.**

**Act 3 — The Throne.** The endgame: ascend the divine ladder to the **Primordials** and the empty seat of power. The final choice resolves your whole run.

## Branching

Driven by your **morality grid (Order⇄Chaos × Purity⇄Corruption)** + **faction allegiance**, which harden through play. They determine which gods you ally with vs. dethrone, which regions open, which endings unlock, and **which god you become.**

## Endings

- **Ascension (9):** become one of the grid-gods (Lawgiver, Iron Throne, Devourer, Free Wild, …) per your axes → **seeds the Succession** (your god becomes the next run's / your friends' world-boss).
- **The God-Maker:** refuse the throne; rule a pantheon of gods you built. Human, sinister, mortal to the end.
- **The Unmaking (true/secret — Unbound path):** climb only to **kill the Primordials and end the Succession itself** — no more gods, ever. A bittersweet liberation; the cycle stops with you.
- Per-faction flavored variants on each.

## Throughline & tone

Funny-grim throughout; the **Succession truth** is the dark heart (you are speedrunning someone's tragedy). Friends' ascended gods appear as **invaders/bosses** (async Succession). Every triumph is also a small horror — and the game knows it, and makes a joke, and means it.

## Ties
Acts gate §World regions, §Factions, and §Bosses. Endgame fuses the Character ladder + the Succession. Competitions & rivals pace Act 1–2 (§Bosses/Rivals).
