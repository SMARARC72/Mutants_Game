**Status:** roundtable-locked (orchestrated) · chances built in `loot_engine.py` · **Last updated:** 2026-06-27
**Principle:** *the slow, gentle, generational path to power — the Tamer's mirror of the Lab.*

## Three ways to get a creature (each feeds your morality)

| Method | Flavor (grid) | Feel | Chance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Befriend** | Pure / mercy | commune & in-encounter choices (feed, spare, soothe); slow but starts with high bond | bond-boosted |
| **Trap** | neutral / dominator | a capture device; reliable, impersonal; starts low-bond/hostile | flat, higher base |
| **Summon** | Creator | grow one from harvested essence in the Lab | a Lab op, not a catch |

## Capture flow

Weaken in battle (**low HP → higher odds**) *or* satisfy befriend conditions, then roll `capture_chance` = base(method) × tier × HP × bond × **gear** × morality-fit (validated numbers in §Loot — e.g. a T2 at 20% HP, fully geared = 70%; a god at 10% HP ≈ 7%).

## Husbandry (after the catch)

- **Taming:** bond grows through use and care; gear speeds it. Bond gates evolutions, Lab-consent, and autonomy (the bond-does-three-jobs system).
- **Care:** what you feed it and *where* you raise it shapes gene expression — and nudges your morality (how you raise it is who you are).
- **Breeding:** two creatures → **egg**; incubation; **dominant/recessive gene + IV inheritance**; rare-gene chase (gear-boosted, 10%→30%); **environmental gene-expression** (the region tints the offspring); full lineage recorded in the creature-soul.

## The husbandry endgame

Wide ±35% genome means **breeding to surface rare recessives and raise IV ceilings** is a deep min-max chase. Lineage tracking + selective breeding over generations = the "perfect specimen" / shiny-gene hunt — and the entry fee for **Breeding Shows** (§Bosses/Competitions).

## Tamer ↔ Creator complement

Acquisition/husbandry is the **slow, gentle, bond** path; the **Lab** is the **fast, dark, build** path. Both produce creatures — your *ratio* between them writes your morality grid and your ending.
