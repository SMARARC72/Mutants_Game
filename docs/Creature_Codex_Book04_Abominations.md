# MUTANTS_GAME — Creature Codex · Book 04 · Taboo Abominations

**Scope:** AB-001…AB-048 (48 net-new taboo-fusion / chimera / god-organ-graft / reanimant / cross-force horrors). All Organic, Rank Legendary (six are God), apex.
**Status:** Book04 complete — AB-001…AB-048 done.
**Method:** funny-grim · stats from live `stat_engine.py` (copied verbatim from dossier) · **NET-NEW, NO ART YET** — every Art field is `TBD (pending art)`; design anchored on dossier `theme`/`force`/`role` intent. These are forbidden-lab fusions whose force-blends are deliberately *opposed* poles — that opposition is the taboo, and the instability is the point.

**Design throughline — "the world hunts you for making this":** none of these were born; all of them were *committed*. Each is two forces that were never meant to share a body, stitched, grafted, reanimated, or welded together by someone who has since been excommunicated, exiled, or eaten. They have opinions about their makers.

**Families introduced:**
- **Splicebrood** — chimera-splice abominations; two beasts (or a beast and a weather) sewn into one disagreeing body. (AB-001, AB-009, AB-017, AB-025, AB-033, AB-041)
- **Graftborn** — god-organ grafts; a stolen divine organ keeps beating inside borrowed meat that can't afford it. (AB-002, AB-010, AB-018, AB-026, AB-034, AB-042)
- **Schismflesh** — cross-force abominations built on a fault line between opposed poles; they hold the contradiction and make it your problem. (AB-003, AB-011, AB-019, AB-027, AB-035, AB-043)
- **Reliquant Dead** — reanimant horrors; corpses re-lit on the wrong fuel, running on a force their lives never used. (AB-004, AB-012, AB-020, AB-028, AB-036, AB-044)
- **Forbidden Weld** — taboo-fusions joined at the seam where two laws of the world fail to agree. (AB-005, AB-013, AB-021, AB-029, AB-037, AB-045)
- **Latticeborn** — flesh-lattices; living tissue grown over an ordering frame until the frame started having preferences. (AB-006, AB-014, AB-022, AB-030, AB-038, AB-046)
- **Amalgam Choir** — soul-amalgams; many smaller things crushed into one larger thing that remembers all of them, badly. (AB-007, AB-015, AB-023, AB-031, AB-039, AB-047)
- **Entropy-Touched** — entropy-mutants; subjects that kept mutating after the experiment ended and never found a reason to stop. (AB-008, AB-016, AB-024, AB-032, AB-040, AB-048)
