### UI-ICON-S01 — Force emblems (6) + rank badges (4)  (10 images)

> One prompt → 10 assets.

**PROMPT (copy-paste):**

UI-GLYPH STYLE (Mutants_Game): each is a bold, readable emblem/symbol on a TRANSPARENT background — clean silhouette, subtle inner glow and bevel depth, more graphic than painterly, crisp at 48px, no text. Force-colored where noted. Game-HUD iconography.

Layout: grid of 10 separate UI glyphs on a transparent background, identical size and stroke weight.

Force palette: Gaia = earthen brown / stone-grey / moss-green · Ouranos = sky-blue / white / silver · Cosmos = white-gold / soft violet / azure · Chaos = ember red-orange / magenta / smoke-black · Eros = verdant green / warm gold / rose · Thanatos = violet-black / bone-white / sickly green

Assets:
1. **Gaia force** — heavy layered-crag/tortoise-shell rune, earthen brown-green, solid and immovable
2. **Ouranos force** — three swept slipstream curves with a spark, sky-blue/silver, fast and weightless
3. **Cosmos force** — concentric ringed mandala with a central calm eye, white-gold/violet, ordered
4. **Chaos force** — asymmetric shattered-star burst leaking ember light, red-orange, broken
5. **Eros force** — a sprouting seed-heart with two leaves, verdant green-gold, alive
6. **Thanatos force** — a small skull wreathed in a single violet flame-wisp, bone-on-black
7. **Wild rank badge** — simple bronze laurel around a pawprint
8. **Legendary rank badge** — ornate silver star-crest with sharp points
9. **God rank badge** — radiant gold halo-crown emblem
10. **Primordial rank badge** — cosmic black-sun monolith emblem, faint starfield
