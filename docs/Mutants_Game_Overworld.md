# MUTANTS_GAME — Overworld & Exploration (v0.1)

**Status:** roundtable-locked (orchestrated) · **Last updated:** 2026-06-27
**Principle:** *a tile-based world that is always hiding one more thing — and one more way to break it.*

---

## Structure

Classic **tile-based 2D top-down** overworld. The **8 force-regions + Threshold hub** connect by paths and **gates** locked behind rank / story / faction / **traversal upgrades** (metroidvania-style: some areas stay sealed until you have the right creature, gear, or rank to cross).

## Region as force-climate

Every region's tiles carry a **force-climate**. It:
- **weights wild encounters** toward that region's forces,
- **pushes gene-expression** of any creature raised, bred, or mutated there (the §Lab / §Husbandry / leveling tie — same egg, different region, different soul),
- deepens with distance — **deeper = deadlier, higher-tier, rarer spawns** (legendaries lurk in the deep & hidden).

## What you do out there

Wild encounters (force-weighted, tier-scaled) · talk to **NPCs**, shops, faction embassies · work **altars** (Lab/sacrifice nodes), **breeding grounds**, **arenas**, **bounty boards** · loot **ruins** & nodes for parts/kits/gear · get ambushed by roaming **rivals** (the nemesis system).

## Reality-warping zones (Alice / the Sunder)

Tiles where the rules **bend**: inverted force-advantage, shifting layouts, size/scale distortion, nonsense-logic puzzles. High-risk, high-reward — rare mutagens, taboo creatures, secret routes. The game's "anything can happen" pressure valve.

## Hidden & secret

Gated by exploration, keys, grid-alignment, or faction standing → **secret bosses**, unique gear & creatures, deep lore. This is the engine of *"there's always more to discover, mutate, combine."*

## Cycles

A **force-tide** cycle (and/or day/night) rotates spawns, events, and which gods and zones are active — replay texture and a reason to revisit.

## Ties
Regions = §World. Encounters → §Acquisition. Gene-expression → §Lab / §Stat-spine. Roaming rivals → §Bosses. Gating → §Loot & Character rank. Warped tiles = the Sunder/Unbound (§Factions).
