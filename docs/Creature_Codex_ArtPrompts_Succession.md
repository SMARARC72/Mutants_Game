# Mutants_Game — Phase B ART PROMPTS · THE SUCCESSION (Dead Gods)

Petrified prior-run champions returned as world-bosses.

---

### DEAD-S01 — Succession Dead Gods (world-bosses)  (8 images)

> One prompt → 8 creature designs on one sheet.

**PROMPT (copy-paste):**

STYLE (lock to the Mutants_Game canon — match the existing storybook/montage sheets): painterly digital illustration, mature-occult fantasy creature-design, full-body 3/4 view, characterful expressive faces, detailed but clean linework, rich slightly-desaturated palette, soft drop-shadow, on a warm off-white/parchment background. Funny-grim tone: each creature has an opinion and a body count — eerie but characterful, never gory, never generic. One single image: a clean numbered grid, each creature clearly separated, uniform lighting, sizes relative to power.

Layout: 2×4 grid, numbered 1–8.


Force palette legend for this sheet:
  · Chaos: ember red-orange, magenta, smoke-black; jagged asymmetric silhouette, glowing cracks, sparks  
  · Cosmos: white-gold, soft violet, pale azure; glowing ordered sigils, halos, crystalline facets, serene symmetry  
  · Eros: verdant green, warm gold, rose; blossoms, vines, branch-antlers, radiant warm life-glow  
  · Gaia: earthen browns, stone-grey, moss-green; heavy rocky plating, roots, lichen, tusks  
  · Ouranos: sky-blue, white, pale cyan, silver; aerodynamic, wind-curled fur/feathers, lightning glints  
  · Thanatos: violet-black, bone-white, sickly green; soul-flame wisps, exposed bone, tattered shroud, hollow glowing eyes

Creatures:
1. **The Lawgiver, Twice-Crowned** (Gaia/Ouranos · world-boss · god) — a petrified former-champion world-boss — an armored revenant-god of cracked stone and soul, Gaia-aligned; palette: earthen browns, stone-grey, moss-green, wind/spark accents; divine awe-scale, radiant and ornate, reality bending faintly around it. Vibe: "Some past run crowned a stone king and crowned him again to be sure, and the world has been politely waiting for him to leave ever since."
2. **The Foreseen, Ever-Early** (Ouranos/Cosmos · world-boss · legendary) — a petrified former-champion world-boss — an armored revenant-god of cracked stone and soul, Ouranos-aligned; palette: sky-blue, white, pale cyan, silver, sigil/glass accents; imposing legendary-boss scale, intricate and intimidating. Vibe: "A champion who once flew so fast it lapsed into precognition, ascended on the strength of always-acting-first, and came back the same: insufferably early."
3. **She Who Audits the Storm** (Cosmos/Chaos · world-boss · god) — a petrified former-champion world-boss — an armored revenant-god of cracked stone and soul, Cosmos-aligned; palette: white-gold, soft violet, pale azure, ember/crack accents; divine awe-scale, radiant and ornate, reality bending faintly around it. Vibe: "A run-winner who reconciled the two things that hate each other most — perfect order and pure ruin — and ascended holding both like a referee holding two players apart."
4. **The Gardener of Wildfire** (Chaos/Eros · world-boss · legendary) — a petrified former-champion world-boss — an armored revenant-god of cracked stone and soul, Chaos-aligned; palette: ember red-orange, magenta, smoke-black, bloom/leaf accents; imposing legendary-boss scale, intricate and intimidating. Vibe: "It tended a riot until the riot bore fruit, won a run on the theory that growth and ruin are the same verb said twice, and ascended grinning."
5. **Engine of the Long Goodbye** (Eros/Thanatos · world-boss · god) — a petrified former-champion world-boss — an armored revenant-god of cracked stone and soul, Eros-aligned; palette: verdant green, warm gold, rose, bone/soulfire accents; colossal self-ascended bronze forge-god (Talos-class), divine machine. Vibe: "A machine-champion built to love its squad and keep them alive, which it did, right up until it ascended and decided the kindest love is a clean ending."
6. **The Sexton-King of the Deep Loam** (Thanatos/Gaia · world-boss · legendary) — a petrified former-champion world-boss — an armored revenant-god of cracked stone and soul, Thanatos-aligned; palette: violet-black, bone-white, sickly green, stone/root accents; imposing legendary-boss scale, intricate and intimidating. Vibe: "A champion who buried so many enemies he became the ground they were under, ascended as a hill that grieves, and came back still settling."
7. **Keeper of the Sealed Mountain** (Gaia/Cosmos · world-boss · god) — a petrified former-champion world-boss — an armored revenant-god of cracked stone and soul, Gaia-aligned; palette: earthen browns, stone-grey, moss-green, sigil/glass accents; divine awe-scale, radiant and ornate, reality bending faintly around it. Vibe: "A run-winner who never lost a single point of ground and ascended as the fact of the ground itself, sealed and sigil-bound so nothing could ever take it."
8. **The Squall That Kept the Crown** (Ouranos/Chaos · world-boss · legendary) — a petrified former-champion world-boss — an armored revenant-god of cracked stone and soul, Ouranos-aligned; palette: sky-blue, white, pale cyan, silver, ember/crack accents; imposing legendary-boss scale, intricate and intimidating. Vibe: "A champion who won the only way it ever knew — hitting first, hardest, and before anyone agreed the fight had started — and ascended on pure tempo."
