### CON-S01 — construct foundry build-lines (frame→engine→colossus)  (9 images)

> One prompt → 9 creature designs on one sheet.

**PROMPT (copy-paste):**

STYLE (lock to the Mutants_Game canon — match the existing storybook/montage sheets): painterly digital illustration, mature-occult fantasy creature-design, full-body 3/4 view, characterful expressive faces, detailed but clean linework, rich slightly-desaturated palette, soft drop-shadow, on a warm off-white/parchment background. Funny-grim tone: each creature has an opinion and a body count — eerie but characterful, never gory, never generic. One single image: a clean numbered grid, each creature clearly separated, uniform lighting, sizes relative to power.

Layout: 3×3 grid, numbered 1–9.

EVOLUTION NOTE: creatures grouped as a line (baby→mid→apex) are the SAME species evolving — keep shared palette & silhouette DNA, escalating size, detail and menace across the three.

Force palette legend for this sheet:
  · Cosmos: white-gold, soft violet, pale azure; glowing ordered sigils, halos, crystalline facets, serene symmetry  
  · Gaia: earthen browns, stone-grey, moss-green; heavy rocky plating, roots, lichen, tusks  
  · Ouranos: sky-blue, white, pale cyan, silver; aerodynamic, wind-curled fur/feathers, lightning glints

Creatures:
1. **Cogframe Mk.I** (Gaia/Cosmos · sentry · T1 frame) — a brass clockwork sentinel of exposed gears; palette: earthen browns, stone-grey, moss-green, sigil/glass accents; compact starter chassis. Vibe: "A squat clockwork watchman built to do exactly one verb — *remain* — and it does so with the deadpan dignity of a thing that has never once wondered why."
2. **Wardengine Mk.II** (Gaia/Cosmos · war-frame · T2 engine) — a brass clockwork sentinel of exposed gears; palette: earthen browns, stone-grey, moss-green, sigil/glass accents; upgraded mid-frame, more armor & power. Vibe: "The Cogframe, re-bored and up-plated until it stopped being a watchman and became an argument."
3. **Bulwark Colossus** (Gaia/Cosmos · siege-colossus · T3 colossus) — a brass clockwork sentinel of exposed gears; palette: earthen browns, stone-grey, moss-green, sigil/glass accents; towering colossus chassis. Vibe: "The foundry's final word on the verb *stand* — a four-storey clockwork keep that walks, barely, and would rather not."
4. **Quickbrass Skein** (Ouranos/Cosmos · sentry · T1 frame) — a polished brass automaton; palette: sky-blue, white, pale cyan, silver, sigil/glass accents; compact starter chassis. Vibe: "A skittering lattice of brass cogs and wound wire that runs *fast* and stops *never* — a sentry that patrols a perimeter forty times a minute and logs each lap with a tiny, satisfied click."
5. **Galeworks Engine** (Ouranos/Cosmos · war-frame · T2 engine) — a polished brass automaton; palette: sky-blue, white, pale cyan, silver, sigil/glass accents; upgraded mid-frame, more armor & power.
