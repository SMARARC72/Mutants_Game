### WILD-S01 — biomes — abyssal anglers; desert nomads; subterranean delvers  (9 images)

> One prompt → 9 creature designs on one sheet.

**PROMPT (copy-paste):**

STYLE (lock to the Mutants_Game canon — match the existing storybook/montage sheets): painterly digital illustration, mature-occult fantasy creature-design, full-body 3/4 view, characterful expressive faces, detailed but clean linework, rich slightly-desaturated palette, soft drop-shadow, on a warm off-white/parchment background.
