# MUTANTS_GAME — Creature Codex · Book 05 — The Succession (Dead Gods)

**Scope:** DG-001…DG-024 (24 net-new world-boss entries — petrified prior-run champions). 24 entries.
**Status:** Book05 complete — DG-001…DG-024 done. *(Matches the Vol01 gold-standard bar.)*
**Method:** funny-grim · stats copied verbatim from the pre-computed dossier (`stat_engine.py`) · **net-new — no art yet** (Art field = `TBD (pending art)`; design anchored on dossier intent, not montage).

> **Design intent — the endgame loop (design bible §3C):** a finished run snapshots your champion + ascended pantheon into a **Dead God** that seeds the *next* run's apex boss. Every entry here is a former player-character-made-god — a prior run's winner, petrified and returned as the obstacle. "The pantheon is a graveyard of winners." Each carries the "you will become one of these" undertone, and the funny-grim wink: gods who are bored, bitter, or quietly delighted to be back on the board. Names are throne-names / titles of dead champions (varied structure), all `summon` (they return, or are invaded into your run). Evolution line for all = **Succession — Dead God (world-boss / NG+ seed)**.

> **Coordination flags for the systems session (do not silently fix — your call):**
> 1. **Art pending:** all 24 are Phase B net-new with no art generated. Force/role reads are anchored on the dossier intent + stat shape, not a montage. Confirm against art when it lands.
> 2. **Construct apex:** DG-005, DG-011, DG-017, DG-023 are Rank=God **Construct** (machine-champions ascended). Flagged in their NOTES — these are the foundry-class of the dead pantheon.
> 3. **Acquisition:** every Dead God is `summon` per the loop (they are seeded/invaded in, not caught). No befriend/trap in this book by design.
