## How factions work

Nine factions, each tied to a region + god and a fixed spot on the **morality grid** (Order⇄Chaos × Purity⇄Corruption — the Character-ladder axes). Each is a **clan** you can court, join, compete in, or war against.

- **Standing** per faction (gated, threshold-based like notoriety): **Stranger → Associate → Sworn → Champion → Hand** (their chosen).
- Higher standing unlocks that faction's exclusive **gear, creatures, skills, breeding lore, and quests.** Aligning **shifts your grid position**; their rivals turn hostile.
- Your final grid position decides your endgame allies and which throne is yours (→ Story, Character ladder).

## The clan loop (competitions)

Earn standing through **quests, deeds, and competitions.** Factions run **leagues** (battle, breeding, lab-craft), and the neutral **Arena at Threshold** hosts cross-faction **tournaments**. Becoming a faction's **Hand** grants a signature reward (a unique god-organ, a legendary creature, or a force-power) — and paints a target on you for the rest. *(Full rival/competition systems → §Bosses, Rivals & Competitions.)*
