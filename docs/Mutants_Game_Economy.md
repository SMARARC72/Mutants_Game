# MUTANTS_GAME — Economy & Items (v0.1)

**Status:** roundtable-locked (orchestrated) · **Last updated:** 2026-06-27
**Principle:** *death funds creation — souls are the only currency the gods respect.*

---

## Currencies

| Currency | What it is | Buys |
|---|---|---|
| **Drachma** | mortal coin | ordinary shops, consumables, common gear |
| **Essence** | distilled **souls** (the soul economy) | Lab ops, summons, rituals, **ascension** — sourced from defeated/sacrificed creatures |
| **Ichor** | rare divine currency | dropped by gods/legendaries; buys divine gear & **god-organs** |
| *Renown* | faction standing (soft) | faction-store exclusives |
| *Corruption* | a **cost**, not spendable | the price tracked on you & your creatures |

## Item taxonomy

- **Gear** (§Loot) — player equipment, 5 slots.
- **Parts / Kits** (§Lab) — organs, gene-vials, cores, scrap (ingredients).
- **Skill-vials** (§Skills) — teach / rank up skills.
- **Consumables** — capture devices, mutagens, heals, stat-tonics, incubators, cleanses.
- **Key items** — traversal & story.

## Markets

Region shops · faction stores (standing-gated) · the **black market** (contraband parts, taboo tech, hot god-organs — draws heat/notoriety) · crafting (Iron Guild / your Lab) · the Arena token store.

## Sources & sinks

- **Sources:** battles, competitions, selling parts/creatures, ruins, bounties, breeding shows.
- **Sinks:** gear, parts, skill-vials, Lab ops, breeding/incubation, **ascension rituals**, faction bribes, black-market premiums.

## The soul-economy thread (the keystone)

Defeated and sacrificed creatures distill into **Essence**, the fuel for the entire Creator engine and the climb to godhood. **Death literally funds creation.** Permadeath (§Battle) feeds the Graveyard *and* the wallet — the funny-grim loop made economic.

## Ties
Parts → §Lab. Gear → §Loot. Vials → §Skills. Essence → Lab + ascension (Character ladder). Black market → §Factions (High Table / Pale Court). Prices scale with rank + notoriety.
