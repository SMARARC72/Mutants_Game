# MUTANTS_GAME — World & Mythology (v0.1)

**Status:** roundtable-locked (orchestrated) · **Last updated:** 2026-06-27
**Principle:** *the world is a graveyard of gods, and you came to rob it.*

---

## Premise

The gods are mostly **dead.** Some were killed, some ascended and walked out, and when they fell their bodies became the **land** — their spilled force still soaks everything and keeps birthing creatures. The thrones of the **Pantheon** stand empty, or occupied by things that used to be people. You're a nobody with a talent for the old arts — *catching, breeding, splicing, and remaking* the force-born beasts — and an ambition the priests call blasphemy and you call **a career plan: take a throne.**

The catch (the **Succession**): every god you'll fight was once a mortal who did exactly this. The Pantheon is a graveyard of *winners*. Ascend, and you join it — and become the wall for whoever comes next, including your friends.

## Cosmology — the six forces are the physics

The 3 opposed axes — **Cosmos⇄Chaos · Eros⇄Thanatos · Gaia⇄Ouranos** — are the substrate of reality. The **6 Primordials** are those forces at full purity: the oldest, near-unkillable cosmic beings, and the ceiling of Apotheosis. Every creature, region, and god is a *blend* of the six.

## How the world reacts (ties to locked systems)

- **The Succession reshapes regions:** a region tilts toward the gods made or killed within it. Kill a region's god and its force runs **wild** (new creatures, hazards, a power vacuum).
- **Your morality grid + notoriety gate content:** Order/Chaos and Purity/Corruption open or close factions, regions, and questlines (Character ladder).
- **Environmental gene-expression:** raising/breeding/mutating a creature in a region pushes its genome toward that region's force (the Lab + leveling).
