# MUTANTS_GAME — Side Quests (content build)

**Author:** quest-design pass · **Last updated:** 2026-06-28
**Source canon:** `Content_Generation_Brief.md` (tone, system-fit) · `_CANON_RATIFICATIONS.md` (currency glyphs ₯✶◈, gear-slot map, Mordathun) · `_NAME_RECONCILIATION.md` (faction-leader truth) · `regions.md` (11 regions) · `factions_npcs.md` + `regional_cast.md` (quest-giver NPCs) · `story_quests.md` (the 24 main quests — these **complement**, never duplicate).
**Voice:** funny-grim, mature-occult. Dread is bedrock; wit is the relief valve. *If a line makes you laugh and then slightly regret laughing, it's on-tone.*

**Principle (inherited):** *the world is a graveyard of gods, and you came to rob it.* The main spine is the climb to a throne; these are the **errands you run on the way up** — the bounty board, the breeding pen, the rumor that won't sit still, the cheerful cursed merchant. Bite-sized, characterful, each gating a real mechanic.

---

## How to read a side quest

Per entry:
- **ID · Title · Type · Region · Giver** (a canon NPC: faction leader, Hand, or regional-cast face).
- **Objective** — the one-line ask.
- **Branch** — 1–2 forks (grid-tagged where it nudges Order⇄Chaos / Purity⇄Corruption).
- **Gate / Reward** — the explicit mechanic it opens: an item (with slot + price in ₯✶◈), a standing tier, a Lab unlock, a capture target, or currency. Drawn from the locked engines; no invented stats.
- **Hook** — the funny-grim flavor beat.

**Conventions (inherited from canon):**
- **6 forces / stats:** Gaia(Bulk) · Ouranos(Celerity) · Cosmos(Ward) · Chaos(Spike) · Eros(Vitality) · Thanatos(Bane). Opposed (taboo to fuse): Cosmos⇄Chaos · Eros⇄Thanatos · Gaia⇄Ouranos.
- **Currencies:** Drachma **₯** · Essence **✶** · Ichor **◈**.
- **Gear slots → boost:** Relic→capture · Tool→lab · Vestment→combat · Charm→tame · Glyph→breed.
- **Standing ladder:** Stranger → Associate → Sworn → Champion → Hand.
- **8 verbs:** Strike · Drain · Ward · Mend · Hex · Rouse · Summon · Gambit. **Statuses:** Petrify · Shock · Seal · Wither · Bloom-rot · Madness · Corruption (the meta-meter).

**Type legend:** `[BOUNTY]` hunt/capture a marked creature · `[HUSBANDRY]` breed/raise/bond · `[LORE]` uncover a region's secret · `[ERRAND]` raise faction standing · `[ABSURD]` the funny-grim oddball.

> These sit *beside* the 24 authored main quests in `story_quests.md`. Where a main quest already gates a mechanic (e.g. the Mend line via "The Mercy That Holds," reanimation via "The First Withdrawal"), these side quests gate **adjacent or lesser** rewards — sub-recipes, single items, befriend targets, standing ticks — and never re-open a main-spine gate.

### SQ-01 · "The Confident Pigeon" · `[ABSURD]` · Threshold · Giver: **Whistle** (T-2, pickpocket apprentice)
- **Objective:** A street child's "legendary" — a very confident pigeon — has survived three encounters and gained a scar. Whistle, the kid's reluctant accomplice, begs you to enter the bird in a *real* Arena warm-up so the child will finally stop selling tickets.
- **Branch:** **Fight it honestly** (it loses in one hit; the child is crushed, then *defiant*, and the pigeon — somehow — bonds to *you* out of spite). · **Throw the match** (let the pigeon "win"; the legend grows, the child becomes briefly insufferable, Whistle profits, you gain a future rival seed).
- **Gate / Reward:** completing it registers the **Confident Pigeon** as a one-off **befriend-eligible capture target** (T1 Ouranos, joke-tier stats, the *only* creature in the game with the trait "Unearned Swagger"). Pays **30 ₯** either way. Throwing the match nudges grid toward Chaos (you fed a delusion); fighting honest nudges nothing but earns the bird.
- **Hook:** *"It's got a* scar* now,"* Whistle whispers, awed. *"Real one. Got it* somewhere*. The kid named it. The kid's selling* tickets*. I just— I can't be part of a* fraud* this size unless it's at least a* little* real. Make it a little real, climber. For my* conscience*. I have one. It's* small*. It's* screaming*."*

### SQ-02 · "What the Tent Keeps Count Of" · `[LORE]` · Threshold · Giver: **Madam Cessil, the Threshold Astrologer** (rare 4th-wall)
- **Objective:** Cessil's tent "keeps count" of something she won't name. She'll tell you what — for a price you pay in a small, true thing about yourself — if you first retrieve the *other* deck from her mute twin, "Other-Cessil," in the tent's mirror.
- **Branch:** **Pay the price** (answer her one true question honestly; she reads you a prophecy that is unsettlingly specific about a choice you haven't made yet — a genuine foreshadow flag). · **Lie to her** (she knows; she lets it stand; the prophecy comes back *wrong* on purpose, and the wrongness is its own warning).
- **Gate / Reward:** unlocks the **Threshold Oracle service** (a repeatable, cheap, *probabilistic* hint at your next region's Legendary force-type — genuinely useful, legally non-binding). Truth-answer grants the **Tin Star of the Watched** (Relic-tier · no force · +small capture-luck; *"someone is rooting for you, or against you; the effect is identical"*), **2 ◈**.
- **Hook:** Other-Cessil hands you a card: *"YOU HAVE BEEN HERE 1, OR 7, OR 41 TIMES. THE TENT KEEPS COUNT. THE TENT IS TIRED."* Cessil, watching, sighs: *"She talks to the one* behind* you. I talk to* you*. Between us we've got the whole* room* covered. ...don't turn around. It's* polite* not to. It* prefers* not to be* seen* counting."*

### SQ-03 · "Number Forty's Last Memo" · `[ERRAND]` · Threshold · Giver: **"Number Forty," the Faceless Embassy-Courier**
- **Objective:** Forty has a sealed memo he can't deliver — the recipient embassy keeps *moving the door*. Run it for him, to whichever of the Nine the seal belongs to (the seal is smudged; you'll have to *read* the faction's wax-mark to know).
- **Branch:** **Deliver it sealed** (faith kept; the recipient faction's standing ticks up one rung, your choice of which). · **Read it first** (you learn a small cross-faction secret — a rumor that opens a different side quest — but Forty's *trust* drops, and he stops handing you the good runs).
- **Gate / Reward:** delivering grants **+1 standing tick** with the faction of your choosing (toward Associate; cannot exceed it) and unlocks the **Embassy-Courier errand loop** (repeatable low-stakes standing-grind runs across the Nine). Reading it instead unlocks a **rumor lead** feeding SQ-11 or SQ-19. Pays **20 ₯**.
- **Hook:** *"I carry for all* nine*,"* Forty pants, already turning to go. *"No loyalty, all* discretion*. I've held the* Steward's* secrets and the* Greenmother's* and* Nael's*— and forgot them* all*,* professionally*, the* instant* I delivered. ...this one I* can't* deliver. The door keeps* leaving*. You've got* legs* and* time*. I've got* neither* and a* reputation* to* protect*. Mostly the* reputation*."*

- **Objective:** Dree sold a "blessed" melon — *technically* alive, *technically* grateful — and the buyer returned it, weeping, because it *purrs* and *will not be eaten*. Dree needs you to find the melon a *home*: a creature that'll bond with it, or a Bloomwarden who'll take it in, or a quiet patch of soil to plant it before sundown (*"don't make eye contact past sundown"*).
- **Branch:** **Plant it** (Pure-lean mercy; it grows into a small, devoted Eros plant-familiar that follows your party one zone, purring). · **Feed it to a creature anyway** (the creature gains a temporary Eros-Vitality buff and a *deeply* guilty expression; Dree is delighted; grid nudges Corrupt a hair — you ate a grateful thing).

Feeding it grants a single **Overripe Draught** (consumable · +Eros/Vitality regen one battle). Pays in *kind words* (free) per Dree's pricing; **15 ✶** if you insist on coin.
- **Hook:** *"Eat it, plant it, or* befriend* it — my produce does* all three*, sometimes* whether you choose or not*!"* Dree beams. *"This one* purrs*. That's not a* defect*, climber, that's* value*. A grateful melon doesn't* spoil* — it* waits*. ...it waits a* long* time. Be* nice* to it. The fruit is* listening*. The fruit is* always* listening."*

### SQ-05 · "Six Petals, True-Bred" · `[HUSBANDRY]` · Verdant Glut · Giver: **Hearthward Ona Bramblekind** (Bloomwarden Hand, husbandry tutor)
- **Objective:** Ona wants to prove the soft creed *works*: breed a Budwarden line through to a **Boughtender** carrying the recessive "six-petal" trait (a purely cosmetic mark the Bloomwardens treasure), raising the offspring *in the Glut* so the ground tints them Eros.
- **Branch:** **The slow way** (selective breeding over generations + environmental gene-expression in the Glut; Stoneblooded would approve, Ona weeps with pride). · **The Garran way** (Old Garran offers a fringe-mutation shortcut "to make it stronger, so it lives longer" — a Revel act in a kindly voice; faster, but the trait comes up *wrong*-colored and Ona's standing cools).
- **Gate / Reward:** the slow way unlocks the **husbandry bond-growth boost** (raises bond-rate on Eros creatures raised in the Glut) and grants **Eros' Unspent Heartbeat**-adjacent **Charm-tier** gear: the **Hearth-Knot Charm** (Charm · +tame-luck on Vitality creatures), **400 ₯ · 20 ✶**. It's not* stronger*. It doesn't* fight* better. It's just* true*, and* ours*, and it took us* generations* to coax it up out of the* dark* of the* blood*. Garran'll offer you a* shortcut*. ### SQ-06 · "The Bloom That Won't Bury" · `[BOUNTY]` · Verdant Glut · Giver: **Sister Wenlow, Bloomwarden Hospice-Keeper** (V-2)
- **Objective:** A wild **Greenwatcher** (Eros/Thanatos, jungle-stalker apex) has been dragging *living* Bloomwarden patients out of the Last-Bloom Hospice and "planting" them in the deep fringe — not to kill, but to *fold them into the cycle* early. Wenlow wants it *stopped*, and — because she's a Bloomwarden — *not killed* if you can manage it.
- **Branch:** **Befriend / pacify it** (Pure; a high-tier befriend under combat conditions, using Mend/soothe; Wenlow is grief-struck with relief). · **Put it down** (faster, cleaner; the hospice is safe but Wenlow won't look at you, and a Bloomwarden funeral for the *Greenwatcher* follows, which you are not allowed to skip).
- **Gate / Reward:** befriending registers the **Greenwatcher** as a **befriend-eligible capture target** (Eros/Thanatos apex, high-bond start) and grants the **Hospice-Tender's Vestment** (Vestment · +corruption-resist, +small Mend potency), **350 ₯ · 25 ✶**. Killing it pays **600 ₯** but no befriend unlock and a Bloomwarden standing dip.
- **Hook:** *"It's not* killing* them,"* Wenlow says, frayed. *"That's the* worst* part. It* loves* them. It thinks it's doing them a* kindness* — getting them into the soil while they're still* warm* enough to* take*. ...the Glut taught it* mercy*, climber. Its* own* mercy. And now I have to ask you to* stop* a thing for being* exactly* as* gentle* as the rest of us. I* hate* this faith. I* believe* it with my* whole heart*. Both. Always* both*."*

### SQ-15 · "The Map That Was True Once" · `[LORE]` · The Sunder · Giver: **The Cartographer of Nowhere, Wess Mapless** (#36)
- **Objective:** Wess maps the Sunder; the Sunder disagrees. - **Branch:** **Confirm the fixed point** (Hewes's ten-foot circle of enforced normalcy; report back, and Wess can finally anchor *one* line of his map — a small, real victory over entropy). · **Tell him there's nothing** (a mercy-lie, or the truth if Hewes's circle has shrunk to nothing by the time you arrive; Wess keeps drawing anyway, because drawing is the relationship).
- **Gate / Reward:** confirming unlocks a **Sunder navigation aid** (Wess's anchored map reveals one *stable* secret-route through the warp — a traversal convenience in the reality-bending region) and the **Cartographer's Charm** (Charm · +tame on Chaos creatures, *"chaos can't fake a bond — probably"*), **300 ₯ · 16 ✶**.
- **Hook:** *"I map the* Sunder*. The Sunder* disagrees*,"* Wess says, already re-drawing. *"We've reached an* understanding*: I draw, it* laughs*, I draw* again*. It's a* relationship*. It's the* longest* one I've got. ...there's a* fixed point* out there, climber. A place the chaos* can't* eat. I can* feel* it.
