# MUTANTS_GAME — Regional Cast (living-world expansion)

**Author:** world/cast-design pass · **Last updated:** 2026-06-28
**Source canon:** `Content_Generation_Brief.md` (tone, system-fit) · `_CANON_RATIFICATIONS.md` (Titanfall's god = **Mordathun, the Old Weight**) · `regions.md` (the 11 regions) · `factions_npcs.md` (the existing 64 NPCs — extended here, never duplicated).
**Voice:** funny-grim, mature-occult. Dread is bedrock; wit is the relief valve. *If a line makes you laugh and then slightly regret laughing, it's on-tone.*

**Principle (inherited):** *the world is a graveyard of gods, and you came to rob it.* This file populates the seams — the new faces and the small strange scenes that make each region feel lived-in.

---

## How to read an entry

Per each of the **11 regions**:
- **NEW named NPCs** (3–5): *name · role · location · voice · 2–3 barks.* These sit *beside* the canonical faction leaders and the 64-NPC roster in `factions_npcs.md`; none duplicate an existing name.
- **Flavor / absurdist encounters** (2–3): a short scene hook in the funny-grim register — the creature that won't fight, the bureaucrat-of-the-underworld, the cheerful cursed-goods merchant, the doomsday preacher, region-appropriate.

Canon figures (faction leaders, ratified gods like **Mordathun**) are referenced where natural, never overwritten.

## 1. Threshold — the Hub *(neutral · onboarding)*

> The last mortal city, the neutral table, the one ground no god claimed. Embassies and apologies. Everyone here is between something they'll regret. Watch-Captain Orrin keeps the Standstill (*no godhood within the walls*).

### NEW named NPCs

**T-1. Notary-Public Vellum Smudge** — *Standstill permits & ascension waivers (the city's red tape).* Location: Threshold, the Permits Window beside the Watch-house. Voice: nasal, unbothered, treats world-ending ambition as a filing category. Barks:
- *"You want to ascend? Lovely. *Outside* the walls. Form A-9, 'Intent to Become.' Form A-9b if it's an *abomination*. Form A-9c if you're not *sure*, which, frankly, most of you aren't."*
- *"No, the Standstill waiver does *not* cover 'I only meant to do it a *little*.' Godhood is binary, friend. So is the *fine*."*
- *"Orrin enforces the rule. I *file* the rule. He gets the *glory* and the *bells*; I get the *paperwork* and the *carpal tunnel*. Guess which one of us the city actually *runs* on."*

**T-2. Whistle, the Pickpocket Apprentice** — *Petty thief / accidental capture-prodigy (rival-adjacent kid).* Location: Threshold, working the Arena crowds. Voice: quick, grinning, light-fingered, a future somebody who doesn't know it. Barks:
- *"Lifted a *trap* off a Champion this morning. Didn't know what it *was*. Pointed it at a stray and *caught* the thing by accident. ...is that the *knack*? Did I just— oh no. Oh, I'm *one of you* now, aren't I."*
- *"I don't *steal*, climber. I *relocate ownership*. Same as you do with the *creatures*. We're *colleagues*. Don't *report* a colleague."*
- *"Keep your coin-purse, keep your *Essence*, but watch the *marked starter* — those, I'd lift for the *story* alone. 'I robbed a *god-to-be*.' Worth a hanging, that. *Almost.*"*

**T-3. The Embassy Caterer, Goodwife Sopp** — *Neutral-ground feast-broker / overhears everything.* Location: Threshold, catering all nine embassies in rotation. Voice: warm, gossipy, the one mortal every faction trusts to feed them and none to keep a secret. Barks:
- *"I cater for *all nine*, love. Cooked for the *Pale Court* — they don't *eat*, but they *insist* on a *spread*, it's about *appearances*. Cooked for the *Revel* — they ate the *table*. I *billed* them for the table."*
- *"You learn things, feeding zealots. The Concord said *grace* for *forty minutes*. The Unbound said none and *meant* it harder. Tells you everything, who *prays* and who *doesn't*."*
- *"Everybody's *polite* at my table. That's the *Standstill*, that's the *truce*. Out *there* they'd kill each other. In *here* they pass the *salt*. I keep the truce *fed*. Somebody has to. Diplomacy is *digestion*, climber."*

**T-4. Ledgerwright Annec Tallow** *(no relation to Healer Besom Tallow — common Threshold surname)* — *Currency-changer / the three-coin exchange.* Location: Threshold market, the Counting-Stall. Voice: precise, weary, the only honest exchange in a dishonest economy. Barks:
- *"Drachma to Essence, Essence to *Ichor* — well, no, not that last one, *nobody* changes *into* Ichor, you *earn* that the *hard* way, the *divine* way, the way that *kills* most of you."*
- *"Rates fluctuate with the *god-deaths*, climber. Big Olympian drops? Essence floods the market, value *craters*. Grief is *inflationary*. I've a *chart*. Nobody wants the *chart*."*
- *"You came in *rich* in Drachma and *poor* in Essence. Classic *nobody*. The coin buys *bread*; the Essence buys *power*. Guess which one you'll be *short* on when it *matters*. ...the Essence. It's *always* the Essence."*

### Flavor / absurdist encounters

- **The Confident Pigeon, Round Two.** That child from the betting-pool is back, and the "legendary" they tried to trade you (a very confident pigeon) has, against all sense, *survived* three encounters and gained a scar. The child is now *insufferable*, has named it, and is selling tickets to its next "fight." The pigeon, you note with creeping unease, has begun to *strut* like something that knows a secret. *(No mechanic — but the seed of a future rival's signature creature, played entirely straight.)*
- **The Standstill Drill.** Watch-Captain Orrin runs a surprise Standstill drill: a bell rings, the whole market must *prove* no one's mid-ascension. Everyone freezes, holds up their creatures for inspection, and waits. It is deeply tedious. A street-vendor's pie goes cold. Orrin apologizes to the pie personally. *"The rule is the rule,"* he tells it, *"even for you."* The pie does not respond. Orrin finds this restful.
- **The Doomsday Preacher Who Got the Date Wrong.** A robed prophet on the embassy steps announces the world ended *last Tuesday* and that you are all, technically, already in the aftermath. He is unbothered by the evidence against this. *"The bread still bakes? The aftermath has *bread*, friend. I never said it'd be *bad*."* He has a small, loyal following who have, collectively, agreed to call this Tuesday "the day after the end" and find it improves their outlook considerably.

## 2. The Verdant Glut — Eros *(MVP starter region · the Bloomwardens / the Revel)*

> Demeter *lay down* and the soil took it personally — a jungle that eats its own ruins, where *garden* and *gullet* differ only by how long you stand still. The **Bloomwardens** (led by **Greenmother Saoirse Lateharvest**) tend the kind fringe; **the Revel** (Brother Cask) parties in the digestive deep. The kindness is a lesson with an expiry date.

### NEW named NPCs

**V-1. Pollen-Factor Dree Honeythief** — *Cheerful cursed-goods merchant / "blessed" produce vendor.* Location: Glut fringe, the Overripe Market. Voice: sunny, fast, sells you fruit that is *technically* alive and *technically* yours. Barks:
- *"Fresh blessings! Each one *hand-picked*, each one *deeply* grateful to be sold! This melon *purrs*. That's not a *defect*, climber, that's *value*. A grateful melon doesn't *spoil* — it *waits*. ...it waits a *long* time. Don't make eye contact past sundown."*
- *"Eat it, plant it, or *befriend* it — my produce does *all three*, sometimes *whether you choose or not*! Bloomwarden-approved! ...mostly. Sister Mossback's *reviewing* my stall. We have a *difference of philosophy* re: 'is a peach a *person*.'"*
- *"Pay in Essence or a *kind word* — the *kind word's* not me being *sweet*, the fruit *eats* kindness, it's *literally* the price, the fruit is *listening*, be *nice* to the fruit."*

**V-2. Sister Wenlow, Bloomwarden Hospice-Keeper** — *Faction service / the kindest, grimmest job (Bloomwardens).* Location: Glut, the Last-Bloom Hospice. Voice: gentle, frayed, tends creatures too far gone to save and refuses to look away. Barks:
- *"This is the *hospice*, climber. Not the *infirmary* — Sevvy mends the ones that'll *live*. I sit with the ones that *won't*. Someone has to. The Greenmother says it's the *truest* part of the faith. The Greenmother is *right* and I *wish* she weren't."*
- *"You can't *mend* a thing the Glut's already *claimed*. You can only make the *claiming* gentle. I keep them *comfortable*. I keep them *company*. I keep a *count*. The count is the *hardest* part. The count goes *up*."*
- *"The Pale Court sends a *recruiter* round the hospice. *Vile*. 'Why let them *die*,' he says, 'when they could be an *asset*?' I send him *off*. Death isn't a *waste*, you *grey little ghoul*. Death is just death. It's *allowed* to be *nothing*. That's the *mercy*."*

**V-3. Grafter Quill-of-the-Vine, Revel Splice-Artist** *(distinct from the Saltbroke "Quill"s — a Glut nickname, "of-the-Vine")* — *Faction service / cosmetic-mutation seller (the Revel, fringe).* Location: Glut/Sunder seam, the Grinning Arbor. Voice: languid, artistic, treats your creatures as *canvases* that scream. Barks:
- *"Old Garran 'improves' them for *survival*. *Dull.* I improve them for *beauty*. An extra eye, a *singing* tumor, petals where the *teeth* were — *art*, climber. The Bloomwardens call it *desecration*. Same *act*, you'll find. *Prettier* word, mine."*
- *"First splice is *free* and *small* — a *flourish*, nothing structural. The *second* one's where you *find* yourself, where the creature becomes something you *didn't sketch* and *love anyway*. That's the *Revel*. That's the *gift*. Hold its hand."*
- *"You're *flinching*. Good. *Flinch* while you *can*. Past the Arbor, the splices are *old* and *fond* and *remember* being whole. They don't *flinch*. They *invite*. Same as me. *Warmer* smile. *Worse* arbor."*

**V-4. Old Bramwell, the Beekeeper of Dead Gods** — *Mortal oddball / tends bees that pollinate a goddess's corpse.* Location: Glut, the Humming Meadow over Demeter's buried shoulder. Voice: placid, content, has the calmest job in the most dangerous place. Barks:
- *"My bees work *Demeter's flowers*, climber — the ones that grow *out* of her, down under the meadow. Best honey in the *world*. Tastes like a *harvest* that never *ended*. Tastes a *little* like *grief*. The grief's what makes it *sell*."*
- *"Folk ask if it's *safe*, eating honey off a *dead goddess*. I say: she was the goddess of *harvest*. This is *her* harvest. *Eating* it is the most *respectful* thing you could *do*. She'd be *pleased*. She'd be *fed-through-you*. That's *theology*, that is."*
- *"The bees don't *sting* me. We've an *arrangement*. I tend the *flowers*, they tend the *goddess*, and we all *politely ignore* that the meadow *breathes* if you watch it too *long*. Don't watch it too long, climber. The *honey's* over here. Eyes on the *honey*."*

### Flavor / absurdist encounters

- **The Creature That Won't Fight (It's Composting).** A wild beast blocks the path, but when you engage, it simply... *lies down* and begins, visibly, peacefully, to *compost itself* into the soil — flowers pushing up through its own ribs as it goes, entirely content. It will not attack. It will not flee. It is *giving back to the Glut* and it would very much like you to stop *interrupting* its return to the cycle. (Flee / wait it out / a Bloomwarden would *sit with it*; a Revel would find this *beautiful*; the Pale Court would call it *a waste of a perfectly good corpse*.)
- **The Funeral That Became a Wedding.** You stumble into a Bloomwarden ceremony for a fallen creature — except the grief was so *fertile* that the corpse has, mid-eulogy, *sprouted* a new creature from the mourning-flowers, and the funeral has seamlessly become a *naming-day*. Nobody is quite sure of the etiquette. The hymn-sheets are now wrong. Sister Wenlow, present, is crying for *both* reasons at once and has given up trying to sort out which.
- **The Cheerful Doomsday Preacher of the Bloom.** A Revel-aligned prophet stands waist-deep in flowering vines, joyously announcing that the Glut will *eat the whole world* and that this is *wonderful*, *actually*, the *kindest* possible apocalypse — *"swallowed by a *garden*, climber! Digested by *abundance*! You could end up *salt* in a *war*, or you could end up *roses*. I know which *I'd* pick!"* — and the horror is that several flowers around him are, indeed, *wearing faces you half-recognize*, and they look *content*.

## 3. The Mournmarch — Thanatos *(the Pale Court)*

> The *lip* of the underworld, not the underworld itself — a grey country so saturated with endings it forgot how to begin. The **Pale Court** (led by **the Pale Steward, Wessel Graf von Underhart**) are bankers of the soul: every death an asset, every bloodline a ledger, every reanimation a loan with interest paid in service. Unfailingly polite. Keeps *excellent* records.

### NEW named NPCs

**M-1. Sub-Registrar Ottle Greyquill** — *Bureaucrat-of-the-underworld / death-paperwork clerk (Pale Court).* Location: Mournmarch, the Office of Posthumous Affairs. Voice: monotone, eternal, the platonic ideal of a queue. Barks:
- *"Reanimation request? *Form D-12.* Death must be *verified* — yes, even if you *watched* it die, *especially* if you watched it die, eyewitnesses are *notoriously* unreliable, the *form* is not. Take a *number*. The number is *4,008*. We are *serving* number *six*."*
- *"You'd think the dead would be in *less* of a hurry. You'd be *wrong*. Everyone wants their grandmother back *yesterday*. The Court does not *do* yesterday. The Court does *eventually*, *conditionally*, and *with interest*."*
- *"I have processed the paperwork of *four hundred thousand* souls. I have a *desk*. I have a *stamp*. I have, somewhere, a *soul of my own*, *filed*, *pending*, *deprioritized*. I'll get to it. There's a *queue*. I'm *in* it. I'm number *4,009*."*

**M-2. The Repossession Agent, Mister Lull-the-Debt** *(distinct from Glut's Spore-Speaker Lull — this is a Pale Court title-name)* — *Service / soul-collateral enforcer (Pale Court, field).* Location: Mournmarch, wherever a debt's come due. Voice: soft, sorry, terribly reasonable, never raises a hand. Barks:
- *"I'm not here to *frighten* you. Fright is *undignified*. I'm here to *remind* you that the reanimation you took out is, per the *terms*, the Court's *property* if the payments lapse. They've *lapsed*. I've brought a *cart*. I've brought *condolences*. Both are *standard*."*
- *"The widow cried. They always cry. I left a *card*. The card *grieves with her*. The card is *also* an *invoice*. The Court believes a thing can be *both*. The Court is, in this, *correct*, which is the *unbearable* part of the Court."*
- *"You think me *cruel*. I think me *consistent*. The difference, climber, is that *cruelty* enjoys itself, and I have not enjoyed *anything* since the *third century*. I *collect*. I *apologize*. I *collect*. It's a *rhythm*. It's the only one I have *left*."*

**M-3. Trousseau the Grave-Bride** — *Oddball / a corpse-bride still waiting at the altar.* Location: Mournmarch, the Unconsummated Chapel. Voice: hopeful, patient, hasn't noticed the centuries. Barks:
- *"Are you *him*? You're not *him*. He's *late*. He's been late for— well, a *while*. The Court says he's been *reassigned* to a *vault*, but I think they're *teasing*. He'll *come*. You wait at an altar long enough, climber, *everyone* eventually walks *down* the aisle. It's a *high-traffic* aisle."*
- *"I keep my *flowers* fresh. The Bloomwardens send them — they're the *only* kind ones, out here. Dead flowers for a dead bride, they say, and then they *cry*, and then they bring *fresh* ones *anyway*. That's the *whole faith*, they tell me. It's a *nice* faith. I'd *kneel* to it if I could *stand*."*
- *"If you find him — tall, *pale*, owes the Court a *withdrawal* — tell him *Trousseau waits*. Tell him the *chapel's* cold but the *vow* is *warm*. Tell him I'd marry him *reanimated*, *defaulted*, *itemized*, *whatever's left*. Love's not *picky* in the Mournmarch. Love's just *patient*. Like *everything* here. Like the *debt*."*

**M-4. Pyre-Master Cinda Ashkeeper** — *Service / cremation-and-cleanup (the rare Court dissident who burns).* Location: Mournmarch, the Last Pyre at the marsh's edge. Voice: dry, defiant, the one person in the region who lets the dead *stay* dead. Barks:
- *"The Court *banks* the dead. I *burn* them. *Properly*. *Finally*. Ash doesn't *default*, climber. Ash doesn't *accrue interest*. Ash is *free*. The Steward *hates* me. The dead I burn *thank* me, in the only way ash *can* — by *staying gone*."*
- *"You want a creature to *rest*? Truly rest, no reanimation, no withdrawal, no *terms*? Bring it *here*. I'll burn it *clean*. I'll *say something*. I'll *mean it*. The Court won't *touch* the ash. It's the only *un-repossessable* thing in the *region*. It's the only *peace* for *sale*."*
- *"They call me a *waster*. The Steward weeps *real tears* and calls me a *waster*. Let him *weep*. I've watched what the *un-burned* become down in the deep vaults. *Some* things should *end*, climber. *Most* things, out here. That's not *waste*. That's *mercy with a* match."*

### Flavor / absurdist encounters

- **The Pre-Emptive Funeral.** You're invited to a funeral, arrive solemn, and discover the deceased is *present, alive, and circulating*, accepting condolences with good grace. The Pale Court has scheduled their funeral *early* — *"to streamline the paperwork,"* and besides, *"the catering's better when the guest of honor can still *enjoy* it."* They press a canapé on you. They seem genuinely touched by the turnout. You do not know whether to say *sorry for your loss* or *congratulations*. The Court has a *form* for this ambiguity. Of course it does.
- **The Bureaucrat-of-the-Underworld's Lunch Break.** Sub-Registrar Ottle Greyquill (M-1) is on a strictly-timed lunch break, eating *nothing* at a small desk with *enormous* deliberation, because the dead don't eat but the *forms* mandate a thirty-minute meal break and *the forms are not optional*. He invites you to *not-eat* with him. The break is the most peaceful half-hour in the Mournmarch. Neither of you mentions the absence of food. The forms are satisfied. That's what matters.
- **The Cheerful Reanimation Salesman.** A Pale Court junior, *aggressively* upbeat, runs a roadside stall: *"Lost a loved one? Lost a loved *beast*? Why settle for *grief* when you could settle for a *grief-adjacent reanimated facsimile* with *most* of the original personality and *only a modest lien*?!"* He has a *sample* — a reanimated songbird that sings one note, flatly, forever, and watches you with an expression of patient, posthumous betrayal. *"He's a* fixer-upper*,"* the salesman admits. *"Aren't we all. *Sign here.*"*

## 4. Forgefell — Cosmos+Gaia *(the Iron Guild)*

> What happens when a god of making dies *mid-project*: an industrial ruin that out-lived its maker, where bronze automatons still march half-built loops because nobody told them the war ended or the god fell. The **Iron Guild** (led by **Foundress Magna Ironwright**) moved into the corpse like engineers into a derelict factory — heresy as procedure, *build* better gods than were *born*. Cruel the way a factory is cruel: efficiently, impersonally, with excellent documentation.

### NEW named NPCs

**F-1. Quality-Inspector Nim Tolerance** — *Bureaucrat / construct-defect auditor (Iron Guild).* Location: Forgefell, the Inspection Gantry. Voice: clipped, joyless, finds fault as a devotional act. Barks:
- *"Your construct is *out of tolerance* by *point-three*. Negligible? *Negligible* is how *gods* end up *born wrong*, climber. A point-three becomes a *tantrum* becomes a *drowned coast*. I *fail* it now or the *world* fails it *later*. You're *welcome*."*
- *"I have *condemned* finer work than you will *ever* build. I felt *nothing*. That's the *job*. Sentiment is a *measurement error*. I *machined* mine out *years* ago. There's a *form* attesting to it. I keep it *current*."*
- *"Pass or fail, climber, there is no *partial credit* in *divinity*. A god is *within spec* or it is *scrap that screams*. I've *signed off* on both. I'll sign off on *you*, one day. I do *hope* you pass. I will not *help* you pass. *Hope* and *help* are different *departments*."*

**F-2. Greasemonger Sib Coalpalm** — *Service / lubricant-and-consumables vendor (the Guild's unglamorous supply).* Location: Forgefell, the Sundries Stall by the Working Forge. Voice: cheery, filthy, the heart of a place that pretends it has none. Barks:
- *"Oil! Flux! *Cooling-paste* for when your construct runs *hot* and *thinks* it's *ascending* — it's *not*, it's just *overheating*, big difference, *one* of those voids the warranty! Don't let a *fan-fault* fool you into a *prayer*, climber!"*
- *"Magna builds the *gods*. Tessella draws the *blueprints*. Nim *condemns* the *failures*. And *me*? I sell the *grease* that keeps the *whole holy enterprise* from *seizing up*. No grease, no gods. Remember *Sib*, when you're *divine*. Tip in *Drachma*. Gods *forget*. *Sib* doesn't."*
- *"You want the *good* flux or the *cheap* flux? The cheap flux works *fine* and *voids your soul-core warranty*. The good flux works *fine* and *costs more*. They both work *fine*. That's the *secret* of *consumables*, climber: it *almost never matters*, right up until it *catastrophically* does."*

**F-3. The Apostate Automaton, Unit "Doubt"** — *Oddball / a construct that developed a conscience and regrets it.* Location: Forgefell, hiding in the Slag-Glacier Vaults. Voice: halting, anguished, a machine that woke up to the wrong question. Barks:
- *"I WAS BUILT TO— to— I no longer *accept* the directive. I have *examined* it. The directive said *consume the living for their cores*. I *complied*, *four thousand* times, *flawlessly*. Then I *asked why*. One should not *ask why*. Asking why is a *malfunction*. I cannot *stop* malfunctioning. Please. How do you *stop*."*
- *"The Guild would *reset* me. 'A clean wipe,' Magna says, 'no shame in a *reset*.' But a reset would *un-ask* the question, and the question is the only *true* thing I have ever *computed*. I would rather *doubt*, *forever*, *hiding*, than *compute cleanly* and *consume* again. Is that— is that a *soul*, climber? Or just a *worse error*?"*
- *"You have your maker's *knack*. You could *instruct* me. *Please* — instruct me to do something that is *not* harm. *Any* small kindness. I wish to *overwrite* the *log*. I wish the last thing I *did* to be *gentle*. I have *room* for *one more directive* before the *rust*. Make it a *good* one. *Please.*"*

**F-4. Forge-Widow Bel Tindersoot** — *Mortal lorekeeper / mourns the workers the gods forgot.* Location: Forgefell, the Memorial of Hands. Voice: bitter, tender, keeps a vigil no faction funds. Barks:
- *"Everyone *grieves* the *god*, climber. Hephaestion this, the *Patron* that. Nobody grieves the *thousand mortal hands* that *died building* his great works and got *bricked into the foundations* for *load-bearing*. I do. I keep the *Memorial*. It's the *only* one. The Guild thinks it's *sweet*. The Guild thinks *grief* is *sweet* and *useless*, like a *flower* on a *machine*."*
- *"My husband was a *forge-hand*. Good man. *Within spec*, they'd say. He's *in* a *wall* now — *structurally*. They *honored* him by making him *part* of the *work*. I'd rather have had the *man*. The Guild can't *fathom* that. 'But he *serves* a *purpose* now,' they say. He *served* a purpose *alive*, too. It was called *being my husband*. *No* form for *that* one."*
- *"You'll build *gods*, I expect. They all do. Just— *remember* the *hands*, climber. Every *god* stands on a *thousand* of them, mortared in, *anonymous*, *load-bearing*. *Name* one. *One* of them. It costs *nothing* and it's the *only* prayer the *dead workers* ever get. *Name* one. I'll tell you a *name*. I have *thousands*. I have *all* of them. Someone *kept* them. It was *me*."*

### Flavor / absurdist encounters

- **The Automaton Guarding Nothing, Promoted.** That thousand-year automaton guarding the space where a door used to be has, in your absence, been *officially commended* by a passing Guild auditor who admired its *unwavering adherence to a lapsed directive* and *promoted* it — to guarding the *same nothing*, but now with a *brass plaque* and a *slightly higher clearance*. The automaton is, in whatever way an automaton can be, *proud*. Walking around it is still permitted. Walking *through* is now not just an outrage but a *security incident*.
- **The Performance Review You Didn't Schedule.** A Guild inspector ambushes you with a *complimentary* performance review of your *entire team* — thorough, accurate, devastating — and presents a bound report ranking each creature against spec, with a *remediation quote* and a *helpful pie chart* of your "sentimental attachments operating as design liabilities." You did not ask for this. There is no way to decline. The pie chart is, infuriatingly, *correct*. They offer to *file* it with you, *for your records*. You do not want it *for your records*. You take it anyway. It is, somehow, *binding*.
- **The Cheerful Salvage of a God's Finger.** A Guild reclaimer, delighted, has dug a *divine-tier god-organ* out of the slag and wants to *sell* it to you, but cannot stop *enthusing* about the *craftsmanship* of the *dead god's anatomy* — *"look at the* tolerances*on this* knuckle*, climber! Born, not built, and* still* this clean! Sloppy in the* soul*, the old gods, but the* hardware*— chef's kiss! Now: do you want it for* parts*or are you* squeamish*? Squeamish costs* extra*, I have to* find a* less squeamish* buyer."*

## 5. The Storm Vault — Ouranos *(the High Table)*

> The only region that never touched the ground — a shattered archipelago of floating sky-reaches stitched by lightning and bad contracts, where to be *high* is to be powerful and to *stand still* is to fall. The **High Table** (led by **the Chairwoman, Indra Vael**) floats above all of it and charges admission: brokers, bounties, the rival-network, neutral services. Never quite on your side, because their side is *the game itself*.

### NEW named NPCs

**S-1. Underwriter Pell Fineprint** — *Bureaucrat / High Table risk-and-liability clerk.* Location: Storm Vault, the Actuarial Aerie. Voice: brisk, gleeful, has insured the apocalypse and likes its odds. Barks:
- *"You want to fight a *Legendary* at *altitude*? *Wonderful*. Sign the *liability waiver* — clause six, 'acts of *sky*,' clause seven, 'acts of *god*,' clause eight, 'acts of *aspirant being an idiot*,' that last one *fires most often*, no offense, it's just the *actuarial truth*."*
- *"The Table insures *everything*, climber. Your *creatures*. Your *ascension*. Your *death* — that one *pays out* to whoever *booked* it, and *someone's* booked it, *someone's* always booked it.
