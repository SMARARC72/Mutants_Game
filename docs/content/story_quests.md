# MUTANTS_GAME — Story & Quests (6-Act Authored Spine)

**Status:** authored content · **Source spine:** `Mutants_Game_Story.md` (4 acts) → expanded to 6 · **Last updated:** 2026-06-27
**Voice:** funny-grim, mature-occult. Dread is bedrock; the jokes make the dread land harder.
**Reads:** `Content_Generation_Brief.md`, `Story.md`, `Character.md` (9-god grid + endings), `Factions.md`, `World.md`, `Bosses_Rivals.md`, `Lab.md`, `Creature_Codex_Book05_Succession.md`.

> **Principle (locked):** *a nobody learns the gods were all nobodies once — and decides to be the last one.* Every triumph is also a small horror, and the game knows it, and makes a joke, and means it.

---

## The 6-Act spine (one line each)

- **Act 0 — The Nobody.** Threshold tutorial; you survive a god's mark and the High Table "invites" you to play.
- **Act 1 — The Climb.** Tour the regions, build team + Lab, court factions, Mortal → Demigod; you learn the **Succession truth**.
- **Act 2 — The Deepening** *(new)*. The grid hardens; you become a faction's **Sworn/Champion**, master the **taboo Lab**, and trip the **first Apotheosis** — your own creature ascends and reshapes a region. Demigod → low Titan.
- **Act 3 — The Reckoning.** The Pantheon notices back; you start **killing Olympians and taking their domains**, each kill running a region wild. Titan ascends.
- **Act 4 — The Throne.** Climb past the Olympians to the **Primordials** and the empty seat; the final choice (the 9 / God-Maker / Unmaking) resolves the run. God → Primordial-threshold.
- **Act 5 — The Succession** *(new)*. Post-choice epilogue-act: you **become the wall**. Your ascended self is snapshotted into a **Dead God** that seeds the next age — and you defend (or refuse) the throne against the next nobody.

**Total authored quests: 24** (4 per act × 6). **Quest-givers:** named faction leaders + Hands + recurring rivals. **Mechanics gated:** Lab unlocks (Mutate→Fuse→Build→Mod→Sacrifice→taboo), region access (all 8 + Threshold), capture targets (Legendaries → Olympians → Primordials), gear/god-organs, faction standing tiers (Stranger→Hand), and **Succession triggers** (first Apotheosis, the throne snapshot, the async-invasion seed).

> **Branch convention:** each quest forks on the **morality grid** — **Order** (Lawgiver/Architect/Iron Throne), **Balanced** (Warden/Broker/Plaguelord), **Chaos** (Free-Wild/Reveler/Devourer) — and on **Purity ⇄ Corruption**. Dialogue is tagged `[Dialogic]` for short authored scenes; `[Ink →]` marks where deep branching lore-trees belong.

# ACT 0 — THE NOBODY

**Premise.** You are nobody — a gutter-talent with the old knack for catching and remaking force-born beasts, squatting in **Threshold**, the last mortal city, built in the lee of a dead god's ribcage. The thrones are empty. Force soaks everything. The tutorial teaches the loop (catch, Lab, fight) while the world quietly establishes its central obscenity: *the gods are dead, the seats are open, and someone is going to sit down.* Then you do something only a god-aspirant could — you **survive a god's mark** (a Primordial-tint brand that should have unmade you) — and the **High Table**, the broker-clan that keeps the rules between god-makers, stops pretending you're furniture.

**Key beats.** A back-alley catch of your **starter** under the ribs of the dead Titan the city is named for. Your first **Lab** run on a workbench that used to be an altar — you Mutate something small and it works a little *too* well. A street-godling (a feral force-knot) marks you mid-fight; you should be a smear; you're fine, and *glowing wrong*. **Vael Construct-Nine**, a High Table envoy with a clipboard and no visible soul, materializes to inform you that surviving constitutes acceptance of terms. The Arena's gates open. The grid begins recording.

**Rising grid tension.** Nothing has hardened yet — Act 0 is where the axes are *introduced as a choice you don't know you're making.* The mark can be borne **purely** (sealed, contained, Concord-coded) or **let it spread** (corruption tint, the Lab loves it). Your first three small decisions (spare/harvest the godling, accept/refuse the contract's fine print, name the starter or number it) plant your initial grid coordinates.

**Climax.** You sign the High Table's marker — or refuse it and they **let you** anyway, because refusing is its own answer. Either way Threshold's bells ring you in as a *registered aspirant*, which in this city means everyone now knows exactly how much you're worth dead.

### Quest 0.1 — "The Knack" (tutorial catch)
- **Quest-giver:** **Old Marrow**, the Threshold ratcatcher who taught you the trade (croaks, missing fingers, fond).
- **Objective:** catch your first creature in the Ribcage Undercroft.
- **Branch (grid):** **Pure** — trap it gently (befriend flavor, bond seed). **Corrupt** — wound-and-bind (a faster catch that nicks the creature's Vitality, +entropy). **Chaos** — let it half-eat a rival rat first, catch the winner.
- **Gate:** unlocks **Capture** + the **roster**; flags your first Purity/Corruption tick.
- **Sample lines `[Dialogic]`:**
  - Old Marrow: "Everything down here is somebody's leftover god. Catch the small ones first. The big ones catch *you*."
  - Old Marrow (on a clean catch): "Gentle hands. The gods had those once. Look how that ended for them."
  - Old Marrow (on a corrupt catch): "Works, don't it. Faster. Costs the beast something. Costs you a little more. You'll stop noticing — that's the trouble."

### Quest 0.2 — "Altar Hours" (tutorial Lab)
- **Quest-giver:** **Quintus Slagg**, a Threshold lab-tech who rents bench time (cheerful, asbestos lungs, "all sales final").
- **Objective:** run one **Mutate** on your starter at the converted-altar workbench.
- **Branch (grid):** **Cosmos-precise** path (Order tilt — deterministic, costs Drachma + entropy). **Chaos-wild** path (Chaos tilt — cheap, rolls a surprise trait, small botch chance).
- **Gate:** unlocks the **Lab — Mutate** operation + the entropy/Instability meter; tutorializes the cost ledger.
- **Sample lines `[Dialogic]`:**
  - Slagg: "This bench took prayers for four hundred years. Now it takes specimens and a deposit. Progress."
  - Slagg (precise): "Slow, sure, pricey. The Concord way. You'll fit right in with the people who fold their socks."
  - Slagg (wild, on a botch): "Huh. It's got an extra one of those now. Two, actually. We do not refund extra ones of those."
- `[Ink →]` deep branch: the **history of the altar** (which god it served, how the city converted it) — a readable lore-tree that seeds Concord vs. Unbound feeling.

### Quest 0.3 — "The Mark" (inciting incident)
- **Quest-giver:** emergent (a street-godling, no name yet) → resolved by **Vael Construct-Nine** (High Table).
- **Objective:** survive the godling's brand; choose what to do with the mark.
- **Branch (Purity ⇄ Corruption):** **Seal it** (Pure — a Concord ward-sigil; the mark sleeps). **Feed it** (Corrupt — let the Primordial-tint spread; +corruption, the Lab gains a hidden recipe slot). **Bargain it** (Balanced — let the High Table *appraise* it for a marker advance).
- **Gate:** sets your **starting grid coordinate**; unlocks the **Arena** (Threshold competition hub) and the **High Table** standing track (Stranger).
- **Sample lines `[Dialogic]`:**
  - Vael Construct-Nine: "You were marked by a dead thing and failed to die. Statistically, you are now a problem. The High Table prefers its problems *contracted*."
  - Vael (Pure seal): "Sealed. Tidy. The seal will hold until ambition removes it. Ambition always removes it. We'll keep the appointment open."
  - Vael (Corrupt feed): "You let it in. Bold. The brand is in your marrow now, climbing. We've started the paperwork on what you'll become. Do try to be interesting."
- `[Ink →]` deep branch: the **fine print** of the High Table marker — a genuinely branching contract you can read clause by clause, each clause foreshadowing a faction or ending.

### Quest 0.4 — "Registered" (Act climax / hub unlock)
- **Quest-giver:** **Madam Thessaly Vance**, the High Table's Threshold steward (velvet, ledgers, a smile like a closing door).
- **Objective:** complete your first Arena bracket bout to be entered on the rolls.
- **Branch (grid):** win **clean** (Pure — refuse to harvest the loser; Bloomwarden eyes turn warm). Win **and harvest** (Corrupt — claim the loser's organ; Pale Court eyes turn warm). Win **theatrically** (Chaos — the Revel marks you as fun).
- **Gate:** opens **Act 1** (regional travel from Threshold) + your first **faction-courting** prompts; sets the **notoriety** baseline.
- **Sample lines `[Dialogic]`:**
  - Thessaly Vance: "Congratulations. You're somebody now — which is the worst thing that can happen to a person in this city. Eight regions, nine clans, one empty sky. Off you pop."
  - Thessaly (clean win): "Mercy. The crowd hated it and the Bloomwardens loved it. Both will matter."
  - Thessaly (harvest win): "You took a piece. The Pale Court noticed. Death keeps better records than I do, and I keep *excellent* records."

# ACT 1 — THE CLIMB

**Premise.** You leave Threshold and walk the corpse of the world. Each region is a dead god's body still leaking its force, each ruled (or haunted) by a faction with a theory about how the thrones should be filled. You build a team, deepen the Lab, win competitions, hunt **Legendaries**, and climb **Mortal → Adept → Demigod**. Then, somewhere in the middle — in a vault, a reliquary, an overheard confession — you learn the thing the priests never say out loud: **the Succession truth.** Every god in the empty sky was once a nobody who climbed *this exact ladder.* The Pantheon is a graveyard of winners. You are not breaking the rules. You are the rules, working as designed.

**Key beats.** First region pick (the world opens by alignment — the warm green **Verdant Glut**, the grey **Mournmarch**, the sparking **Forgefell**, depending on whom you courted in Act 0). A **Legendary** hunt as the region's capstone — a named monster-boss guarding a force-node. Your first real **faction league**, climbing a force-themed gym-ladder of trainers. A recurring **rival** introduced — they climb *with* you, rubber-banded to your rank. The mid-act gut-punch: the **Reliquary of Winners**, where a Concord archivist (or a Pale Court mortician, or a drunk Reveler — whoever you're nearest) shows you the truth and watches your face do the math.

**Rising grid tension.** Now the axes *bite.* Courting the **Bloomwardens** (Pure) and the **Pale Court** (Corrupt) at once becomes impossible — standing with one cools the other. The **Iron Guild** wants you efficient; the **Unbound** want you free; the **Concord** want you *lawful*; each tug moves your coordinate and shuts a door behind you. The first faction you anger sends hunters (the notoriety threshold model fires its first discrete world-shift).

**Climax.** You reach **Demigod** and the Reliquary truth lands. The High Table convenes a **Marker Court** — a formal recognition that you are a genuine contender — which is also the moment the *standing* Pantheon (the things on the thrones now) first turns its head toward you. The climb stops being a sport. It becomes a war you started by being good at a game.

### Quest 1.1 — "Greener Pastures, Hungrier Ones" (region access)
- **Quest-giver:** **Mother Sylva Greenrot**, leader of the **Bloomwardens** (Verdant Glut · Demeter) — warm, doomed, smells of loam and grief.
- **Objective:** clear the fungal bloom choking the Glut's eastern terraces; decide the bloom's fate.
- **Branch (grid):** **Pure** — heal and replant (befriend the bloom-beasts; Bloomwarden standing↑). **Corrupt** — harvest the bloom for **Eros gene-vials** (Pale Court/Revel↑, Bloomwarden↓). **Chaos** — let it spread to the next terrace and farm the chaos (Revel↑).
- **Gate:** unlocks **region access: the Verdant Glut** + the **Lab — Mod** operation (augment a living beast with bloom-organs); first **Mend** skill if Pure.
- **Sample lines `[Dialogic]`:**
  - Sylva Greenrot: "We don't butcher here, child. We *tend.* The land is a body and the body is still dying and the least we can do is hold its hand. Will you hold its hand, or harvest it?"
  - Sylva (Pure): "Bless you. You'll get yourself killed being kind, but you'll be *kind* when it happens. That's rarer than it should be."
  - Sylva (Corrupt): "I see. Off to the Court with you, then. Don't bring that back here. The garden remembers who cut it."
- `[Ink →]` deep branch: the **Glut's history** — Demeter's death, why life here grew teeth, the schism between Bloomwardens and the Revel. A sprawling readable tree.

### Quest 1.2 — "The Foreman's Problem" (Lab — Build)
- **Quest-giver:** **Foreman Castor Brail**, leader of the **Iron Guild** (Forgefell · Hephaestus) — soot, prosthetic everything, treats ethics as a tolerance spec.
- **Objective:** reactivate a dead automaton line in Forgefell; build a construct to pass the Guild's intake.
- **Branch (grid):** **Order** — follow the blueprint exactly (Iron Guild↑, clean construct). **Corrupt** — graft a harvested soul-core for extra power (Iron Guild *respects* it, +corruption, instability). **Chaos** — improvise from scrap and see what wakes up (Revel/Unbound nod; botch risk).
- **Gate:** unlocks the **Lab — Build** operation (parts → construct) + **construct blueprints**; **Forgefell** region deepens; **Iron Guild** standing → Associate.
- **Sample lines `[Dialogic]`:**
  - Castor Brail: "The gods were *born.* Sloppy. Random. We can do better — measure twice, sanctify once, bolt the divinity on straight. Build me something that doesn't die of being alive."
  - Castor (Order): "Within tolerance. Beautiful. A god you could file a maintenance ticket against. That's the dream."
  - Castor (Corrupt core): "You put a *soul* in it. Off-spec. Powerful. The thing screams when it's idle — but the numbers, my friend, the *numbers.*"

### Quest 1.3 — "The Guardian at the Node" (Legendary capture)
- **Quest-giver:** **Huntmaster Bram Stoneblood**, of the **Stoneblooded** clans (Titanfall · the Old Weight) — patient, scarred, speaks in seasons.
- **Objective:** track and defeat (or capture) the region's **Legendary** force-node guardian.
- **Branch (grid):** **Pure** — bond it (high-bond capture; Stoneblooded husbandry↑). **Corrupt** — break it and **harvest a god-adjacent organ** for the Lab (Pale Court↑). **Balanced** — defeat and register the kill with the High Table for a **bounty** payout.
- **Gate:** unlocks **Legendary-tier capture** + the **Lab — Sacrifice** operation (consume → ingredients/levels); **Stoneblooded** → Associate; first **god-organ-adjacent** ingredient if Corrupt.
- **Sample lines `[Dialogic]`:**
  - Bram Stoneblood: "The old clans don't catch a Legendary. We *court* it — years, sometimes. You've got an afternoon and an ambition. The Weight will judge which is worth more."
  - Bram (bond): "It chose you. Hold that. A bonded Legendary is worth ten broken ones — and it'll grieve you when you fall, which is more than most gods got."
  - Bram (harvest): "You cut a guardian for parts. The mountain felt that. Walk careful in Titanfall now; the Old Weight keeps grudges longer than you'll keep breathing."

### Quest 1.4 — "The Reliquary of Winners" (Act climax / Succession truth)
- **Quest-giver:** **Archivist Pellos Vane**, of the **Concord** (Astral Tier · Athena/Apollo) — deadpan occult bureaucrat; or, if Corrupt-aligned, **Sister Morrow** of the **Pale Court** delivers the same truth with a mortician's tenderness.
- **Objective:** enter the Reliquary; read the record of the ascended; survive what you learn.
- **Branch (grid):** **Order** — accept the truth as *law* (the ladder is legitimate; you'll climb it correctly → Concord↑). **Corrupt** — accept it as *permission* (if they were nobodies, nothing is sacred → Pale Court/Unbound↑). **Chaos** — laugh at it (the whole pantheon is a cosmic joke and you want the punchline → Revel/Unbound↑).
- **Gate:** opens **Act 2**; raises you to **Demigod**; triggers the first **Pantheon notice** (a standing god marks you); unlocks the **High Table Marker Court** and the cross-region **Arena tournament**.
- **Sample lines `[Dialogic]`:**
  - Pellos Vane: "Observe the record. Every name in the sky has a *before.* A gutter. A debt. A first catch in some undercroft. They were you. Then they sat down, and the chair ate the person and kept the power. We file them as 'Successors.' The honest word is 'meals.'"
  - Pellos (Order): "You understand, then. It is a *system.* Systems can be climbed correctly. Climb correctly. The alternative is the Unbound, and they don't fold their socks."
  - Sister Morrow (Corrupt variant): "Sweet thing. They were nobodies, every one — and they *ate the world* anyway. Nothing up there earned the awe. So tell me: why should *you* hold back?"
  - Player (4th-wall crack, rare): "...So I'm speedrunning someone's tragedy." / Pellos: "We don't use that word here. But yes. And someone will speedrun yours. Tea?"
- `[Ink →]` deep branch: the **full Reliquary** — a deep, optional lore-tree of named prior Successors (mapped to the **Dead Gods**, `Book05_Succession.md`: the Lawgiver Twice-Crowned, the Gardener of Wildfire, the Grudgekeeper of the Burnt Court). Reading a Successor's file foreshadows the exact world-boss you'll face in Act 5 — and quietly previews what *your* file will say.

# ACT 2 — THE DEEPENING  *(new)*

**Premise.** The middle game, where the loop stops being shallow and the price starts being real. You stop *courting* factions and start *belonging* to one — climbing **Sworn → Champion**, taking their signature work, inheriting their enemies. You crack the **taboo ceiling** of the Lab: cross-force **Fusion**, **god-organ grafts**, and — the line you can't uncross — **splicing yourself.** And you trip the **first Apotheosis**: you push one of your own creatures to a pure force-pole and it *ascends* into a minor god that walks out of your roster and **reshapes a region in your name.** This is the first time the Succession is *yours* — the first proof that the graveyard of winners has a fresh plot with your handwriting on the headstone. Rank **Demigod → low Titan.**

**Key beats.** The **Sworn rite** of your chosen faction (a deed only a true believer would do — the Pale Court asks for a sacrifice, the Bloomwardens for a mercy, the Unbound for an arson). The **taboo Lab** sequence: your first **opposed-force abomination** (huge power, the world posts a bounty on it). A **rival's heel-turn** — the one who climbed beside you in Act 1 now defects to your faction's enemy and starts hunting you, *sticky.* The **first Apotheosis ritual**: feeding a creature to a pure pole until it crosses over. And the **self-splice temptation** — the first god-organ that fits *you*, not a beast.

**Rising grid tension.** This is the act where **Purity and Corruption stop being reversible-feeling.** Each taboo operation bolts a Pure ending shut and pries a Corrupt one open; each faction rite drags your coordinate hard toward its corner of the grid. The world starts treating you as a *kind of god-to-be* rather than a promising mortal — NPCs rewrite their lines, the Concord cools if you've spliced, the Pale Court warms, abominations begin to *obey* you if you've fed the corruption. The neutral middle gets lonely on purpose.

**Climax.** Your **first Apotheosis fires.** A creature you made crosses the pole and ascends — and its home region *tilts* (its force runs partly wild, its faction reels, new spawns and hazards bloom). You watch a thing you built become a small god and stop being yours, and the High Table sends a single line of congratulation that reads exactly like a condolence. **The Succession is now live in your save.** (Mechanically: this seeds the *first* Dead-God snapshot fragment — the wall is starting to build itself, and it's wearing your face.)

### Quest 2.1 — "The Sworn Rite" (faction standing → Champion)
- **Quest-giver:** the leader of **your most-courted faction** — e.g. **Lord Severin Ash** of the **Pale Court** (Mournmarch · Hades), aristocrat of endings, collects last words; or **Mother Sylva Greenrot** (Bloomwardens); or **Magister Aurelian Vox** of the **Concord**.
- **Objective:** perform the faction's Sworn rite — the deed that proves you're theirs, not a tourist.
- **Branch (grid):** **Pure (Bloomwarden rite)** — give one of your own creatures its peaceful release rather than let it burnout-feral (a mercy; Eros↑). **Corrupt (Pale Court rite)** — **Sacrifice** a bonded creature for a soul-core on the bloodline-bank altar (Thanatos↑, +corruption). **Order (Concord rite)** — seal a rogue force-node under law (Cosmos↑). **Chaos (Unbound rite)** — burn a standing shrine of the *living* Pantheon (notoriety spikes; the marked god retaliates).
- **Gate:** raises chosen faction to **Champion** (unlocks signature gear/creatures/skills); **shifts your grid coordinate hard**; the faction's **rival clan turns hostile** (discrete world-shift).
- **Sample lines `[Dialogic]`:**
  - Severin Ash: "Anyone can *visit* death. Becoming Sworn means you *spend* something you love and call the receipt 'progress.' Bring me a sacrifice you'll actually miss. Sentiment is the only currency that proves the heart was real before you spent it."
  - Severin (Corrupt rite): "There. Feel that? The hollow where it used to be? That's not loss. That's *room.* We'll fill it with something that doesn't die on you. Welcome to the Court."
  - Sylva (Pure rite): "You let it go *gently.* You could have wrung one more battle from it and you didn't. That's the whole of us, child — we lose on purpose, with grace. The garden has a Champion."
- `[Ink →]` deep branch: each faction's **Sworn-rite liturgy** as a full branching ceremony — every clause a moral fork, every fork logged to the grid.

### Quest 2.2 — "The Line You Can't Uncross" (taboo Lab — Fuse / abomination)
- **Quest-giver:** **Doctor Vesh Quillon**, an Unbound lab-heretic working out of the **Sunder** (the Sunder · Eris) — gleeful, ungoverned, missing the part of the brain that flinches; rivaled by **Foreman Castor Brail**, who'll do the same job *to spec* if you stay Order.
- **Objective:** perform your first **opposed-force Fusion** (Cosmos⇄Chaos, Eros⇄Thanatos, or Gaia⇄Ouranos) — a true abomination.
- **Branch (grid):** **Corrupt/Chaos** — go through with the abomination (huge power; **the world posts a bounty**; +corruption; abominations begin to obey). **Order** — refuse the taboo, build a *compatible* fusion to spec with Brail instead (no bounty, less power, Concord stays warm). **Balanced** — broker the abomination *to the High Table* as a registered exception (you keep it; they keep a marker on it).
- **Gate:** unlocks the **taboo Lab tier** (cross-force Fusion + the world-bounty system); unlocks **command over abominations** if you went through; **the Sunder** region access; **Unbound** → Sworn.
- **Sample lines `[Dialogic]`:**
  - Vesh Quillon: "They call opposed-fusion an *abomination* like that's an argument. It's just two truths the universe swore would never touch, holding hands. The instability? Cosmetic. The bounty? Free advertising. *Fuse it.*"
  - Vesh (on success): "AH — look at it *not making sense!* Gorgeous. The world's going to want it dead, which means the world's finally paying attention. You're welcome."
  - Castor (Order refusal): "Smart. Opposed forces fight forever inside the chassis — you'd spend the rest of its short life patching the screaming. Build *compatible.* A god you can maintain beats a god you have to apologize for."
- `[Ink →]` deep branch: the **taboo-recipe codex** — discoverable opposed-fusion formulas, each with a lore-fragment on the prior aspirant who first dared it (most are dead; one is a Dead God you'll fight).

### Quest 2.3 — "The One Who Climbed Beside You" (rival heel-turn)
- **Quest-giver:** **your Act 1 rival**, now defected — e.g. **Kestrel Dane**, who you out-paced and humiliated, now Sworn to your faction's enemy and *obsessed.*
- **Objective:** survive the rival's ambush; decide their fate (the sticky-outcome fork).
- **Branch (grid):** **Pure** — spare and *recruit* them (they may become an ally/breeding partner). **Corrupt** — break and **harvest** them (their best creature becomes Lab stock; they return worse, or as an Act boss). **Balanced** — defeat and *register* them with the High Table for a bounty (they're contractually bound off your back — for now).
- **Gate:** locks the **rival's sticky state** (ally / nemesis-boss / bounty-bound); a recruited rival unlocks a **co-op breeding line**; a harvested one unlocks a **revenge Act boss** later.
- **Sample lines `[Dialogic]`:**
  - Kestrel Dane: "You were *nobody.* I was nobody *next to you,* which was worse. I found a clan that doesn't lose. Now there's a marker on your back and my name's the ink. Nothing personal. It's *entirely* personal."
  - Kestrel (spared): "...You let me walk. After everything. That's either mercy or a longer game. Gods, I can't tell anymore. Fine. I'm yours. Don't make me regret being alive."
  - Kestrel (harvested): "You took her — my *best* — for *parts*? Then I'm not done. I'll come back as something you can't catch. Count on it. *Count on it.*"

### Quest 2.4 — "First Light" (Act climax / first Apotheosis — Succession trigger)
- **Quest-giver:** **Vael Construct-Nine** returns (High Table) to *witness and notarize* the ascension; **Madam Thessaly Vance** sends the congratulation-that-is-a-condolence.
- **Objective:** push one of your own creatures to a **pure force-pole** and complete the **Apotheosis ritual** — make your first minor god.
- **Branch (grid):** the **pole you choose** stamps the act — ascend toward **Eros/Cosmos** (Pure poles; the new godling blooms/orders its region) or **Thanatos/Chaos** (Corrupt poles; it withers/unmakes its region). **Order** Apotheosis seats a lawful godling (Concord recognizes it); **Chaos** Apotheosis seats a wild one (the Revel throws a festival).
- **Gate:** **TRIGGERS THE SUCCESSION (first instance):** your creature ascends → **reshapes its home region** (force runs partly wild, faction reels, new spawns/hazards) → **seeds the first Dead-God snapshot fragment** in your save (the async-invasion wall begins building); raises you to **Titan**; opens **Act 3**.
- **Sample lines `[Dialogic]`:**
  - Vael Construct-Nine: "Recording. Subject pushed to pole. Subject *crosses.* ...Note for the file: it stopped being yours at the threshold. They always do. Congratulations. You have made a god. The god has already forgotten you. That, too, is the system."
  - Thessaly Vance (the message): "My dear contender — word reaches us that you've seated your first. The whole Table raises a glass. We've also opened a file under your name in the Reliquary. Purely procedural. Do keep climbing. The view from up there is *exactly* as lonely as they say, and we'd hate for you to miss it."
  - Player (rare 4th-wall, quiet): "It looked back at me. Once. Then it didn't." / Vael: "Yes. Mark that feeling. Somewhere, a nobody you'll never meet is about to feel it about *you.*"
- `[Ink →]` deep branch: the **Apotheosis liturgy** — the ritual as a branching tree where the *words you choose to crown it with* become the seed-text of the Dead God you'll one day be (and that a friend's run may invade as a boss). The first time the game writes *your* Reliquary file in front of you.

# ACT 3 — THE RECKONING

**Premise.** The standing Pantheon — the **Olympians**, the things that sit on the thrones *now* — stops watching and starts moving. They are not abstract: they are the **33 god-rank creatures**, named force-vectors, and they would like you to stop. So you do the unthinkable and start **killing them** — and each kill is an **Act boss** that, when it falls, **reshapes the region it ruled.** Kill Demeter and the Verdant Glut's growth runs feral and starving; kill Hades and the Mournmarch's dead stop staying dead; kill Hephaestus and Forgefell's automatons wake up *grieving.* Alliances harden fully along your grid: the gods you spare become patrons, the gods you butcher become martyrs to their factions. Rank: **Titan ascends** as you eat the sky one domain at a time.

**Key beats.** The **first deicide** — a chosen Olympian, picked by your grid (you go for the one your faction hates most). The **domain-claim** afterward: you don't just kill the god, you *take the office* — inherit a fraction of its force-power and its region's allegiance. A **god turns patron** — one Olympian, seeing where you're headed, offers to back you against its rivals (a Faustian alliance). The **factions split** over you — clan wars ignite, and they resolve *partly based on you.* And the **Pantheon's counter-move**: the surviving gods convene and send their best — a Legendary host, a god-organ assassin, your own ascended Act-2 godling *turned against you* by the thrones.

**Rising grid tension.** Now the grid is mostly *locked* — your coordinate has a gravity well, and the act is about *committing.* Order players kill the *chaotic* gods and seat law; Chaos players kill the *lawful* gods and free the wild; Pure players kill *cleanly* (deicide as euthanasia, the gods are suffering on those thrones); Corrupt players kill *greedily* (deicide as harvest, a god-organ is the best Lab stock there is). The Unmaking path first becomes *visible* here as a rumor: that you could climb not to sit, but to **kill the Primordials and end the whole Succession.**

**Climax.** You kill enough of the Pantheon that the **sky has holes in it** — and the **Primordials stir.** The six pure poles, the oldest near-unkillable things, take notice that a mortal has been thinning their children. The High Table calls the **final Marker** — recognition that you are no longer a contender but a *succession event in progress.* The thrones above the Olympians, where the Primordials sit and the empty seat waits, finally open. **Act 4** is the climb past the gods into the godhead itself.

# ACT 4 — THE THRONE

**Premise.** The endgame. You climb past the dead Olympians into the godhead itself — the **six Primordials**, the pure poles, the near-unkillable ceiling of Apotheosis — and the **empty seat** they guard, the throne of thrones that has stood vacant since the last winner walked out. Everything resolves here. Your whole playthrough — every grid tick, every faction rite, every god you spared or ate — has written which god you *can* become, and now you choose: **ascend as one of the 9 grid-gods**, **refuse the throne and rule as the mortal God-Maker**, or take the secret door and **kill the Primordials to end the Succession forever (the Unmaking)**. Rank: **God → Primordial-threshold.** This is the act the whole game has been the joke of, and the act where the joke stops.

**Key beats.** The **Primordial trials** — to reach the seat you must face the pure poles, each a superboss that *is* a force in its totality (the Unmaking path: you must *beat them to death* instead of past them). The **final faction reckoning** — your Champion clan arrives to crown you or stop you, depending on whether your ending is theirs. The **convergence of every thread** — your patron god, your reclaimed/harvested godling, your sticky rival, all present for the last climb. And the **Choice itself**, staged at the empty seat, where the game lays your entire grid before you and asks the only question it ever had: *what kind of god do you become — and do you become one at all?*

**Rising grid tension.** The grid doesn't *rise* here so much as *deliver its verdict.* The 3×3 has narrowed to the one or two gods your axes permit; the act's tension is the gap between **who you meant to be** and **who you turned out to be** — the game shows you both and does not let you pretend they're the same. The **Purity ⇄ Corruption** axis decides the *flavor* of your throne; the **Order ⇄ Chaos** axis decides its *shape*; the **Unbound flag** decides whether there's a throne at all.

**Climax — the three doors.** (1) **ASCEND (the 9):** you take the empty seat and become your grid-god — **Lawgiver, Architect, Iron Throne / Warden, Broker, Plaguelord / Free-Wild, Reveler, Devourer.** The chair eats the person and keeps the power. (2) **THE GOD-MAKER:** you *refuse* the seat, descend, and rule a pantheon of gods *you built* — human, sinister, mortal to the end. (3) **THE UNMAKING (true/secret):** you climb only to kill the Primordials, end the Succession itself, and walk out of a world that will never make another god — bittersweet liberation, the cycle stopped with you.

- **Gate:** unlocks **Primordial-tier access** (the seat / the superbosses); killing a pole on the **Unmaking** path **permanently removes that force's god-seed from the world** (the Succession can never recur through it); passing seats your eventual ascension.

# ACT 5 — THE SUCCESSION  *(new — the post-throne act)*

**Premise.** The signature spine, paid in full: **Apotheosis → the Succession.** You won. You sat (or refused, or unmade) — and now the game turns the knife it has been showing you since Act 0. If you **ascended**, your champion and the pantheon you built are **snapshotted into a Dead God** (`Book05_Succession.md`) — and you become **the wall.** A *new* nobody, somewhere, in the next age or a friend's world, is climbing the exact ladder you climbed, and the apex boss they'll break themselves against **is you.** Act 5 is the inversion of the whole game: you play the *other side* of the Succession — the defending god, bored and bitter and quietly delighted to be back on the board — and you watch a stranger speedrun the tragedy you just lived. The funny-grim heart, fully open: *every triumph was also a small horror, and now you're the horror.*

> **Branch-by-ending (Act 5 has three modes, set by the Act-4 Choice):**
> - **ASCENDED (the 9):** you *are* the Dead God now. You defend the throne against the next climber (async-invasion). Your grid-god determines your boss-form and the Dead-God entry you become.
> - **GOD-MAKER:** no Dead-God seed — instead you persist as a **mortal recurring power**, sending *your built gods* as the next run's bosses while you, the human puppet-master, watch from a throne you never sat in.
> - **UNMAKING:** there is no Act 5 invasion to defend — the cycle is *over.* This mode is the **bittersweet coda**: a quiet, near-empty world, and the single question of whether a cycle that *can't* recur was worth ending.

**Key beats.** The **petrification** — your champion crowned, then stilled, then *seeded* (the moment you become a statue with a grudge). The **first invader** — a new nobody (NPC, or a friend's async character) arrives at *your* throne with a starter and a clipboard, and you fight them *as the wall.* The **recognition** — somewhere in the fight you see them do the thing you did (survive a mark, make a first light) and the game lets you feel it from the *other* end. The **Succession choice** (the act's own fork): do you **crush** the climber and keep the seat cold, **let them past** (become the rare Dead God who *steps aside*), or **break your own petrification** to end *your* link in the chain (a late, costly echo of the Unmaking)?

**Rising grid tension (the inversion).** The grid stops measuring *your* morality and starts measuring **what kind of obstacle you choose to be.** A **Pure** Dead God might guard the seat to *protect* the next climber from the chair's hunger; a **Corrupt** one guards it to *feed.* An **Order** wall enforces the rules of the climb; a **Chaos** wall breaks them to make the climber *earn* it sideways. The axes you spent the whole game setting now read as your *philosophy of being a boss* — the last, strangest expression of who you turned out to be.

**Climax — the chain.** The new nobody either **falls** (the seat stays yours; the age keeps your name on the wall; the Succession rolls on, and the game shows you the *next* nobody already walking into Threshold under your dead-god shadow) — or **wins** (they unseat you, you become the *previous* Dead God, the deep-cut secret boss, and *they* inherit the wall). Either way the final image is the loop made explicit: **a graveyard of winners, and a fresh plot, and someone walking toward it with the old knack and an ambition the priests call blasphemy.** The game's last funny-grim wink: a load-screen line for the *next* run that reads *"the gods are mostly dead — and one of them used to be you."*

### Quest 5.2 — "The First Invader" (defend the throne — async-invasion)
- **Quest-giver:** a **new nobody** (an NPC successor, or a friend's async character) arrives at your seat with a starter and the old knack — *you* are now the quest's antagonist.
- **Objective:** fight the climber **as the Dead God** — the wall the whole game taught you to break, now manned by you.
- **Branch (grid — your philosophy of being a wall):** **Pure** — guard *gently* (you test them, but you *protect* them from the chair's hunger; a wall that wants them to be worthy, not dead). **Corrupt** — guard *greedily* (you want them broken and harvested; the seat *feeds*). **Order** — enforce the climb's rules to the letter. **Chaos** — break the rules; make them earn it sideways and screaming.
- **Gate:** plays the **async-invasion** from the defender's seat (the friends-async loop, inverted); the climber's outcome **rolls the chain forward** — defeat them and the seat holds, lose and you advance to 5.3 as the *deposed* Dead God.
- **Sample lines `[Dialogic]`:**
  - The Dead God (you, Pure): "You made it to the top, little nobody. Good. Now I have to try to kill you — not because I want the seat, but because the seat only keeps the ones who can *take* it. Show me you can take it. Show me you're worth the horror you're about to become."
  - The Dead God (you, Corrupt): "Fresh meat at the summit. They always smell the same — *hope,* and *cheap gear.* Sit if you can. You can't. The seat's *mine,* and I'm so hungry up here. Come closer. Let me see what you're made of, *specifically.* I have a drawer for it."
  - The climber (an echo of you): "You were nobody once. Everyone up here was. I read your file in the Reliquary. ...You were *me.*" — The Dead God (you): "...Yes. Now go on. *Be* me. It's the worst thing I can wish you, and the only thing the chair allows."

### Quest 5.3 — "The Wall's Choice" (the inversion fork)
- **Quest-giver:** the **moment itself** — climber bloodied at your feet (you won 5.2) or you unseated (you lost) — the game asks what *kind* of ending to the chain you are.
