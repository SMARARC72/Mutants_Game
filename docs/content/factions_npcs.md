# MUTANTS_GAME — Factions & NPCs (content)

**Author:** narrative-design pass · **Last updated:** 2026-06-27
**Source canon:** `Mutants_Game_Factions.md` (the Nine, grid, rivals) · `Mutants_Game_World.md` (8 regions + Threshold) · `Mutants_Game_Character.md` (9-god grid) · `Mutants_Game_NPCs_Reactivity.md` (tone, standing model) · `Mutants_Game_Bosses_Rivals.md` (leagues/competitions) · `Mutants_Game_Lab.md` + `Mutants_Game_Acquisition_Husbandry.md` (gates).
**Voice:** funny-grim, mature-occult. Dread is bedrock; the wit is the relief valve. *If a line makes you laugh and then slightly regret laughing, it's on-tone.*

**Standing ladder (all factions):** Stranger → Associate → Sworn → Champion → **Hand**. Higher standing unlocks gear, creatures, skills, breeding lore, quests; aligning shifts your grid position and turns their rival hostile.

**Conventions in this file**
- Each faction: **Leader** (name, title, voice, goal, grid, character paragraph) · **3 Hands/quest-givers** · **standing-tier dialogue** (Stranger → rising → Hand) · **2 gated quest hooks** (the unlocked mechanic stated explicitly).
- **Gate** = the real system a quest opens: a Lab operation/recipe, a capture target, region access, gear, or a standing reward — drawn from the locked engines.
- Grid cells use the canon names: Lawgiver · Architect · Iron Throne / Warden · Broker · Plaguelord / Free Wild · Reveler · Devourer.

## 1. The Concord — *"order is the only mercy that scales"*

**Region · God:** The Astral Tier · Athena/Apollo · **Grid:** Order / Pure (the Lawgiver pole)
**Rival:** the Unbound. **They offer:** Ward & Seal skills, Cosmos creatures, order-gear.

### Leader — **Archon Velleth Sun-Notary, the Last Lawful Voice**
*Title:* First Archon of the Concord; Keeper of the Empty Bench.
*Voice:* serene, exact, exhaustingly reasonable; speaks in clauses; never raises her voice because she has never needed to.
*Goal:* refill the empty thrones *by procedure* — gods reinstated as a cosmic civil service, audited, accountable, never again a tyrant by accident.
*Grid-alignment:* Lawgiver (Order/Pure) — courting her pushes you Order and Pure.

Velleth has not slept in eleven years, which she will tell you is a discipline and not a symptom. She remembers the gods personally — not the myths, the *paperwork*: the broken covenants, the unrenewed mandates, the divine negligence that let a sky-king's tantrum drown a coast. She concluded, with a clerk's terrible calm, that the problem was never the gods but the *lack of process*, and she has spent her life building the office that would govern the next ones. She is kind in the way a good audit is kind — it will ruin you fairly, with full documentation, and feel genuinely sorry. Mortals adore her. Her own Hands are a little afraid of her. When she calls your rise "an irregularity we can regularize," it is the warmest thing she knows how to say.

### Hands / quest-givers
- **Sub-Notary Oren Tallowmark** — *Intake & Standing Clerk.* Voice: brisk, apologetic, drowning in forms; the man who actually files your ascension. "Welcome. You're a hazard. Let's get you a *number*."
- **Warder-Adept Simca of the Eighth Seal** — *Ward-skill instructor.* Voice: monkish, patient, treats every seal like a prayer with load-bearing walls. Teaches you to keep a thing in instead of out.
- **Auditor Halish Greypledge** — *Field Auditor (compliance enforcement).* Voice: dry as chalk, follows you on "spot inspections," secretly the most violent person in the Tier. "I'm not here to fight you. I'm here to *document* you fighting me."

### Standing-tier dialogue
- **Stranger:** *"You smell of three regions and an unfiled mutation. State your business, or be one."*
- **Associate:** *"You returned the borrowed Ward-seal. Unprompted. Do you know how rare that is? I've adjusted your file. Don't make me un-adjust it."*
- **Sworn:** *"You took the lawful path when the wild one was faster and cheaper. The Concord noticed. The Concord *records*. There is a chair here that has your shape."*
- **Champion:** *"The Tier opens its inner seals to you. Walk carefully — every door past this one was built to keep a god *in*."*
- **Hand:** *"You are the Concord's Hand now. When you ascend, you will be the most thoroughly documented god in the sky — and I mean that as the highest compliment I am capable of. Sign here. And here. And, regrettably, here."*

### Gated quest hooks
- **"The Unrenewed Mandate"** — Recover a Primordial-touched Cosmos core from a sealed Astral vault that three rival aspirants are also raiding (a Concord-run **Battle League** bracket gates the door). **Gate:** unlocks the **Ward & Seal skill line** at the Lab/trainer (Simca teaches **Containment Seal** — the prerequisite for safely crafting cross-force *abominations* without the world-bounty firing immediately). Order/Pure deed.
- **"Quiet the Wild Saint"** — A Cosmos **Legendary** has gone feral in the crystal reaches after the Tier's last partial god-death; the Concord wants it *contained, not killed*. **Gate:** completing it under the befriend/non-lethal condition grants **region access to the Astral Tier's inner citadel** *and* registers the Legendary as a **befriend-eligible capture target** (high-bond start). Killing it instead flips Velleth's standing and opens the Unbound's hostile arc.

## 2. The Iron Guild — *"a god that breaks was built wrong"*

**Region · God:** Forgefell · Hephaestus · **Grid:** Order / Tainted (the Architect pole)
**Rival:** the Bloomwardens. **They offer:** construct blueprints, parts/kits, Lab-gear.

### Leader — **Foundress Magna Ironwright, the Hand That Improves**
*Title:* Foundress of the Iron Guild; First Smith of the Empty Forge.
*Voice:* blunt, warm, profane, oil-stained; talks about gods the way a master machinist talks about a beautiful, badly-toleranced part.
*Goal:* build better gods than the ones that were born — engineered, repeatable, *serviceable* divinity. Ethics, to Magna, are a design constraint, not a wall.
*Grid-alignment:* Architect (Order/Tainted) — courting her pushes you Order and a little Tainted.

Magna lost an arm to a god-organ that "wasn't documented properly," replaced it with a better one she made herself, and considers this the founding parable of her entire philosophy. She loves the work with a ferocity that scares people: she will weep over an elegant fusion and then casually describe consuming a living creature for its core, in the same breath, with the same affection. To Magna there is no cruelty in the Lab, only *tolerances* — a thing is either within spec or it is scrap, and scrap is just material that hasn't found its build yet. She thinks the born gods were sloppy, vain, and under-engineered, and she is not wrong, which is the unsettling part. She'll call your monstrous chimera "honest work" and mean it as love. Her one tenderness: she names every construct, and she remembers every one she's had to melt down.

### Hands / quest-givers
- **Journeyman Cael Brassknuckle** — *Blueprint Quartermaster.* Voice: eager, motor-mouthed, sells you the Build recipe and three upgrades you didn't ask for. "It's not 'reanimation,' it's *refurbishment with a soul-core*. Totally different department."
- **Sister-Wright Odya Coldweld** — *Mod & augment specialist.* Voice: quiet, precise, missing the small-talk module; grafts plating onto your living beasts. "Hold still. It only hurts if it's working."
- **Slag-Master Bohm** — *Sacrifice-bench operator (the rendering pits).* Voice: gentle, mournful, runs the place where creatures become ingredients. Hums to them first. "Nothing's wasted here. That's the only kindness I've got. It's a real one."

### Standing-tier dialogue
- **Stranger:** *"You're a tamer. Cute. You stroke them and hope. We *build* them. Come back when you've stopped apologizing to your tools."*
- **Associate:** *"Huh. Clean weld on that chimera. Self-taught? Don't answer, I can see the scarring. The Guild has a bench with your name half-stamped on it."*
- **Sworn:** *"You melt down what doesn't make spec and you don't flinch. Good. Sentiment is the most expensive material there is. Here — a Guild blueprint. Try not to make something that hunts us."*
- **Champion:** *"The deep forges are yours now. Mind the god-core vault — that's not metal cooling, that's a divinity that hasn't agreed to be dead yet."*
- **Hand:** *"Hand of the Iron Guild. You're a *manufacturer* of gods now, not a beggar at their feet. When you ascend, you'll be the first one with a *maintenance schedule*. I'll service you myself. I'll be sorry when I have to."*

### Gated quest hooks
- **"The Patron's Cold Heart"** — Retrieve a still-warm god-core from Hephaestus's collapsed primary forge, contested by a Guild rival who wants it for himself. **Gate:** unlocks the **Build operation tier-up** at the Lab — the **god-organ graft** recipe (divine-tier part on a mortal construct) *and* the Forgefell **Lab-gear** set that lowers Build entropy-cost. Order/Tainted deed.
- **"Within Spec"** — Win a Guild **Lab-Craft Contest** by submitting a fusion that hits a named force/tier spec without tipping into abomination. **Gate:** the win unlocks the **discoverable repeatable recipe** system at Magna's bench (turn a one-off gamble into a mass-produceable formula) and raises standing toward Sworn. Losing by going *over* spec (an opposed-force abomination) wins a different, darker prize and a Bloomwarden grudge.

## 3. The Pale Court — *"death is just power no one's redeemed yet"*

**Region · God:** The Mournmarch · Hades · **Grid:** Order / Corrupt (the Iron Throne pole)
**Rival:** the Bloomwardens. **They offer:** sacrifice & soul tech, Thanatos creatures, reanimation.

### Leader — **The Pale Steward, Wessel Graf von Underhart**
*Title:* Steward of the Mournmarch; Registrar of the Bloodline-Bank; First Mourner.
*Voice:* immaculate, courtly, funereal; the warmth of an undertaker who genuinely loved your grandmother and has already measured you.
*Goal:* run death like an aristocracy with a vault — every soul a deposit, every bloodline an *estate*, nothing truly lost, only *held in trust* against the day it's wanted back.
*Grid-alignment:* Iron Throne (Order/Corrupt) — courting him pushes you Order and Corrupt.

Wessel does not believe in death. He believes in *liquidity*. He is the oldest-seeming person you will meet who is not technically a god, dressed for a funeral that has been going for three centuries, and he treats every creature you've ever lost as an asset still on the books — recoverable, for a fee, with the right paperwork and the right blood. His manners are flawless and his patience is geological and underneath both is something that has stopped finding the difference between the living and the inventory. He is not cruel; cruelty is hot, and Wessel has not been hot in a very long time. He will offer to reanimate your dead starter and mention, gently, the interest accrued. The Pale Court's horror is that it makes *sense* — and that Wessel will weep, real tears, at the funeral of someone he is about to repossess.

### Hands / quest-givers
- **Archivist Mund the Indexed** — *Graveyard registrar.* Voice: whispery, precise, knows where every soul is "filed." Manages your reanimation roster. "Your boar? Vault seven. Mind the *terms*, the terms are the whole thing."
- **Bone-Sexton Halryn Coldmercy** — *Reanimation surgeon.* Voice: tender, clinical, calls corpses "the patient." Rebuilds from your Graveyard. "Death's the easy part. *Manners* — bringing them back *grateful* — that's the craft."
- **Collector Veum** — *Debt-enforcer (repossessions).* Voice: soft-spoken, never threatens, simply *arrives*; the one who collects on souls in arrears. "I'm not angry. I'm *itemized*."

### Standing-tier dialogue
- **Stranger:** *"You grieve them. How quaint. Grief is just an unpaid invoice you've decided to feel instead of settle. The Court settles."*
- **Associate:** *"You sacrificed your own to fuel the work, and you did it *cleanly*, without that nauseating little speech. The Steward keeps a ledger of promising mourners. You're penciled in."*
- **Sworn:** *"You understand now — nothing here is *gone*, only *owed*. Walk the bloodline-bank. Touch nothing that has a lien on it. Everything has a lien on it."*
- **Champion:** *"The deep crypts open. Down there the deposits are *old* — gods' own bloodlines, held against a withdrawal no one's dared make. The Steward thinks you might dare."*
- **Hand:** *"Hand of the Pale Court. When you die — and the powerful die *spectacularly*, it's practically a gala — you will not be lost. You will be *held*. I'll sign the deposit slip myself, with genuine sorrow, and I will look forward to your withdrawal."*

### Gated quest hooks
- **"The First Withdrawal"** — Reclaim a soul-deposit the Court has held since before the gods fell, guarded by what it became while it waited. **Gate:** unlocks the **Reanimate-the-dead Lab operation** (rebuild from your Graveyard) *and* the **soul-core harvest** path on defeated creatures (Thanatos ingredient source). Order/Corrupt deed; firmly tips you toward the Iron Throne.
- **"Letters of Marque, Posthumous"** — Hunt down and "settle the estate of" a rogue rival god-maker who died owing the Court (a High Table **Bounty**, co-signed by the Pale Court). **Gate:** the settlement grants a **Thanatos Legendary capture target** (the rival's loosed signature creature) *and* standing toward Champion; reanimating the rival's *team* from their Graveyard snapshot is the dark-bonus objective that draws notoriety and Bloomwarden hostility.

## 4. The High Table — *"there are no gods, only contracts that haven't lapsed"*

**Region · God:** Storm Vault · Zeus · **Grid:** Balanced (the Broker pole)
**Rival:** nobody — and everybody. **They offer:** bounties, the rival/competition network, neutral services.

### Leader — **The Chairwoman, Indra Vael of the Long Marker**
*Title:* Chair of the High Table; Holder of the House Edge; She Who Books the Bets.
*Voice:* delighted, predatory, generous; a gambler who has already calculated your odds and is *thrilled* for you regardless of how they came out.
*Goal:* keep the game *going* — the Succession is the greatest table ever opened, every aspirant a player, every god a payout, and the House takes its cut of the apocalypse.
*Grid-alignment:* Broker (Balanced) — the High Table is the one faction you can court without committing your grid; they *like* you uncommitted, it's better for the line.

Indra Vael runs the only neutral power in a world of zealots, and she does it by believing in nothing except the *spread*. She "invited" you into the game in Act 0 not out of kindness but because a fresh nobody with a marked starter is *excellent action* — long odds, big personality, the kind of bet the whole Table wants a piece of. She is warm the way a casino is warm: every comfort, every courtesy, every door held open, all of it engineered to keep you playing one more hand. She does not want you to win or lose; she wants the *game*, forever, and a god who ends the Succession is the one thing that genuinely frightens her, because it closes the book. When she toasts your ascension she means it — a god is a *marquee event* — and she has already opened the line on who kills you.

### Hands / quest-givers
- **Croupier Sable Ninefold** — *Bounty broker.* Voice: silken, fast, deals quests like cards. Posts the named-rival hunts. "Got a god-maker three regions over who's *overdue*. Pays in Ichor. Interested? Of course you're interested."
- **Marker-Keeper Doss Halloway** — *Arena registrar & odds-maker.* Voice: gruff, fair, weirdly paternal; runs the Threshold Arena seasons. "Sign the marker. Win, you collect. Lose, the marker collects. Either way the *Table* collects, that's just the geometry."
- **The Accountant (no name given, never the same face twice)** — *Settlements & contracts.* Voice: flat, unhurried, unnerving; the one who pays out and the one who comes to collect. "A contract is just a god you can read. Honor it and you'll never meet me angry. You don't want the other meeting."

### Standing-tier dialogue
- **Stranger:** *"Fresh blood, marked starter, no faction tan-line — oh, you're *gorgeous*. Sit. The first hand's complimentary. They never are again."*
- **Associate:** *"You paid a marker you could've skipped town on. The Table *noticed*. Credit's a kind of faith and you've got some now. Spend it loud."*
- **Sworn:** *"You're booking real action — leagues, bounties, the lot. The House likes a workhorse with a story. There's a private room at the Table; the games in there bite back."*
- **Champion:** *"The long markers open to you now — contracts on *gods*. Sign one and the whole Table watches. Win and you're a legend. Lose and you're a *lesson*."*
- **Hand:** *"Hand of the High Table. You're not a player anymore, sweet thing — you're *house*. You take a cut of every aspirant's rise now. And when you go for your throne, I'll have the biggest line in history open on you, and I'll be cheering, and I'll mean both."*

### Gated quest hooks
- **"The House Always Climbs"** — Win a full **Arena** season at Threshold (the seasonal neutral cross-faction tournament). **Gate:** the title unlocks the **Bounty board** (the named-rival hunt loop — your access to the Wick nemesis system as quests), pays **Ichor**, and grants a neutral **gear-stash** usable across all factions without grid penalty. Balanced deed — banks standing without committing your grid.
- **"Marker on a God"** — Take a **long marker**: a High Table contract to dethrone a named Olympian *to the Table's timetable*, not yours. **Gate:** signing grants early **region access** to that god's region (the Table greases the door) and the **Olympian as an Act-boss capture-or-kill target**; fulfilling it pays a **unique god-organ** from the Table's vault. Defaulting on the marker summons the Accountant as a recurring rival.

## 5. The Stoneblooded — *"the old weight remembers; so do we"*

**Region · God:** Titanfall · (a dead Titan) · **Grid:** Balanced / Pure (the Warden pole)
**Rival:** the Revel. **They offer:** breeding & taming lore, Gaia creatures, bond-gear.

### Leader — **Matriarch Ostrega Deephold, Keeper of the Old Weight**
*Title:* Eldest of the Stoneblooded clans; Bloodmother of the Worldback Herds; Warden of the Slow Way.
*Voice:* slow, granite-calm, dry as a dust of millennia; speaks in the long view and finds your hurry quietly funny.
*Goal:* endure. The gods rose fast and fell faster; the clans abide, breeding strength into the bloodlines one patient generation at a time, until the flashy die out and the *steady* inherit.
*Grid-alignment:* Warden (Balanced/Pure) — courting her pushes you Pure and toward Balance, away from both fanatic poles.

Ostrega is older than several of the factions and considers most of them weather. She raised a Worldback from a Sproutling and outlived three of her own children, and she speaks of both with the same unhurried tenderness — grief, to the Stoneblooded, is just love that has agreed to take its time. She thinks the Lab is *cheating*, the way a flood is cheating: powerful, undeniable, and gone the moment the rain stops, leaving nothing that lasts. The clan way is slower and it is *real* — a recessive gene coaxed up over six generations is *yours* in a way no fusion ever will be. She will not lecture you about it; she'll simply outlast your impatience and let the bloodlines make her argument. Her warmth is the warmth of deep stone: it takes forever to feel it, and once you do, it never leaves, and it has buried armies.

### Hands / quest-givers
- **Herd-Father Bress Gravelhand** — *Breeding-lore keeper.* Voice: gruff, kind, smells of hay and old blood; teaches lineage and the rare-recessive chase. "Anyone can catch a strong one. *Making* a strong one — that's marriage and patience and a little heartbreak. Sit. I'll show you the lines."
- **Tame-Mother Yula Stillhand** — *Bonding & husbandry instructor.* Voice: soft, endless, the calm a frightened beast trusts; teaches the befriend path and care-rituals. "You don't break them. You *outlast their fear*. Same as people. Same as gods, I'd wager, if anyone'd had the patience."
- **Showmaster Cairn Oldoak** — *Breeding Show judge.* Voice: ceremonious, exacting, loves a fine genome the way others love a sunrise. "I don't score the *strong* ones, child. I score the *true* ones. There's a difference and it's the whole world."

### Standing-tier dialogue
- **Stranger:** *"You move fast and you carry a Lab-stink. We'll see if there's anything under the hurry. The stone's in no rush to find out."*
- **Associate:** *"You bred a line instead of buying one. Took you *generations*. Most of your kind can't sit still that long. The clans are watching the slow way you carry yourself."*
- **Sworn:** *"You've raised a true one — bond-deep, clean-blooded, no shortcuts in it. The Stoneblooded know that weight. Come to the deep holds; the old herds will let you near."*
- **Champion:** *"The breeding-vaults open — bloodlines we've kept since the Titan's fall, recessives no flood-maker's ever surfaced. Tend them well. They've outlived everyone who didn't."*
- **Hand:** *"Hand of the Stoneblooded. When you ascend — if you do, the fast way always tempts your sort — you'll be a god that *lasts*, built generation on generation. The clans will still be here after the others' thrones are dust. We'll remember you. We remember everything. It's our only real magic and it's enough."*

### Gated quest hooks
- **"Six Generations Deep"** — Surface a named rare *recessive* gene in a Gaia bloodline through selective breeding, then win the Stoneblooded **Breeding Show** with the result. **Gate:** the win unlocks **bond-gear** (raises capture befriend-odds and tame speed) and the **rare-gene chase boost** at Bress's lineage bench (the 10%→30% recessive uplift). Balanced/Pure deed.
- **"The Old Weight Wakes"** — A Gaia **Legendary** of the corpse-mountains stirs as Titanfall destabilizes; the clans want it *bonded, not broken*. **Gate:** completing it via the befriend/care path grants **region access to the deep holds of Titanfall** and registers the Legendary as a **high-bond befriend capture target**; it also unlocks **environmental gene-expression** tuning (raise offspring in Titanfall to tint them Gaia). Trapping or killing it sours Ostrega and pushes you off the Warden cell.

## 6. The Bloomwardens — *"everything that grows is owed a gentleness"*

**Region · God:** Verdant Glut · Demeter · **Grid:** Pure (Warden-to-Lawgiver edge)
**Rival:** the Pale Court / Iron Guild. **They offer:** Mend skills, Eros creatures, husbandry boosts.

### Leader — **Greenmother Saoirse Lateharvest, Who Buries the Hungry**
*Title:* Greenmother of the Bloomwardens; Tender of the Glut; Last Friend of the Doomed.
*Voice:* warm, lilting, heartbroken; the gentleness of someone who has loved a great many dying things and never once looked away.
*Goal:* tend, heal, befriend — never butcher. Life is sacred even when it's *winning too hard*; the Glut's job is to love the world enough to survive its own fertility.
*Grid-alignment:* Warden→Lawgiver (Pure) — courting her pushes you hard toward Purity; she is the moral conscience of the roster, and she is *doomed* and knows it.

Saoirse is the warmest person in the game and the saddest, because she has chosen the one creed the world is built to punish. The Verdant Glut is not a gentle place — it is fertility turned *menace*, life so abundant it strangles, fungal blooms that eat the healers tending them — and Saoirse loves it anyway, the way you love a child who frightens you. She will not let you butcher a creature in her sight; she will weep over your harvested beasts and she will *mean it* and she will still hand you a Mend-skill afterward, because mercy that gives up isn't mercy. The grim joke of the Bloomwardens is structural: the kindest faction is the most *fragile* one, hemmed by the Pale Court who'd bank their dead and the Iron Guild who'd render them for parts, and every Bloomwarden knows the soft path may not survive the Succession. Saoirse knows it best of all. She tends anyway. That's the whole point of her.

### Hands / quest-givers
- **Mendwright Toval Greenmercy** — *Mend-skill instructor.* Voice: bright, gentle, relentlessly hopeful in a way that's almost a wound. Teaches the heal/soothe line. "We can't out-kill the Court. So we out-*last* them, one mended creature at a time. It's not nothing. Some nights it's everything."
- **Hearthward Ona Bramblekind** — *Husbandry-boost & befriend tutor.* Voice: practical, warm, the no-nonsense aunt; the one who keeps the soft creed *functional*. "Love's a method, not a mood. Feed them right, raise them kind, and the bond does three jobs for you. Sentiment with *results*."
- **Seed-Keeper Wll the Quiet** *(speaks rarely; mostly through the plants)* — *Eros creature-warden.* Voice: hushed, strange, more grove than person; tends the befriendable Eros beasts. "...they like you. That's not nothing, out here. Out here it's a *miracle* with teeth."

### Standing-tier dialogue
- **Stranger:** *"You've got blood under your nails and I can smell which kind. We don't do that here. But you came in instead of around — so. Tea?"*
- **Associate:** *"You spared one you could've harvested. I saw your face do the math and then *refuse* it. That refusal is the whole faith, little one. Welcome to the losing side of it."*
- **Sworn:** *"You mend more than you maim now. The Glut feels it — the blooms lean your way. Walk the deep grove; the gentle things there have learned to fear footsteps, and they don't fear yours."*
- **Champion:** *"The heart-grove opens to you. Demeter's own untended children are in there, half-gods gone feral with grief. We don't kill them. We *sit* with them. Bring patience. Bring tea. Bring your softest creature."*
- **Hand:** *"Hand of the Bloomwardens. Oh, love. You chose the kind path in a world that *bills* for kindness, and you climbed it anyway. If you ascend a gentle god, you'll be the rarest thing the sky has ever held — and the most hunted. I'll worry about you forever. I worry about everything. It's how I love."*

### Gated quest hooks
- **"The Mercy That Holds"** — Talk down (do not kill) a grief-maddened Eros half-god in the heart-grove using only the befriend/soothe path while it tries to bloom you to death. **Gate:** unlocks the **Mend / Soothe skill line** (in-encounter befriend conditions for *hostile* and high-tier targets) *and* the **husbandry boost** that raises bond-growth rate. Pure deed; pushes you toward the Warden/Lawgiver edge.
- **"What the Glut Won't Bury"** — The Pale Court is harvesting Bloomwarden creatures for soul-tech at the Glut's edge; drive them off and *reclaim the living*, not the dead. **Gate:** success grants **region access to the deep heart-grove**, an **Eros Legendary befriend target** (Demeter's loosed child), and a permanent **husbandry uplift** to befriend-method capture odds in the Verdant Glut. Siding with the Court instead opens the inverse Pale Court arc and makes Saoirse grieve you while you still live.

## 7. The Revel — *"the gods died of moderation; we won't make their mistake"*

**Region · God:** Verdant Glut / the Sunder · Dionysus · **Grid:** Chaos / Tainted (the Reveler pole)
**Rival:** the Stoneblooded. **They offer:** mutation catalysts, Chaos creatures, wild gear.

### Leader — **The Vintner, Brother Cask of the Glorious Mutation**
*Title:* Mouth of the Revel; Vintner of the Sweet Rot; First to Change.
*Voice:* effusive, intimate, dangerously charming; a poet drunk on transformation who toasts every horror as a *becoming*.
*Goal:* ecstasy and indulgence and *change for its own sake* — the body is a costume, mutation is the only honest prayer, and the gods fell because they refused to become anything new.
*Grid-alignment:* Reveler (Chaos/Tainted) — courting him pushes you Chaos and Tainted, fast and joyfully.

Brother Cask used to be three different people, he'll tell you, and he liked all of them, and he likes what he is now even more, and he can't entirely remember which parts were original — and he finds this *delicious* rather than horrifying, which is the exact spiritual difference between the Revel and everyone else. He is the most genuinely fun person in the world and the most quietly dangerous, because his joy is *real* and it wants you to drink, to change, to mutate one more time, *just to see*. The Revel's horror is seductive: it doesn't grab you, it *invites* you, and the invitation is so warm and the wine so good that you've grown a third eye before you remember to ask whether you wanted one. Cask loves you immediately and completely and will weep with delight when you finally splice yourself, because to him you'll have *arrived*. He thinks moderation is a slow death and abundance is the only mercy, and his laugh is the last warning you'll get.

### Hands / quest-givers
- **Mixmistress Yarrow Threetongue** — *Mutation-catalyst vendor.* Voice: giggling, fast, sells you the Chaos-wild reagents. "Cheap, potent, *unpredictable* — like all the best things and most of the worst! What're we changing today, gorgeous?"
- **The Ecstatic, Pell Manyhanded** — *Wild-gear & Chaos-creature broker.* Voice: blissed, generous, somewhere past the body; deals in catalyzed beasts. "Took six mutations to grow the hands. Worth every one. Want to hold something that shouldn't *be*? Course you do."

## 8. The Unbound — *"every throne is a wound; we are the salt"*

**Region · God:** The Sunder · Eris · **Grid:** Chaos / (any) — slides Free Wild ⇄ Devourer
**Rival:** the Concord. **They offer:** taboo tech, abominations, Chaos.

### Leader — **The Unmaker, She-Who-Is-Called Nael (and other things)**
*Title:* the Unbound have no leaders; Nael is merely the loudest refusal. Voice of the Sunder; the Salt in the Wound.
*Voice:* quiet, certain, almost gentle; the calm of someone who has seen the whole shape of the thing and decided, without rancor, to end it.
*Goal:* burn every throne; let no god rule again — not refill the pantheon, not improve it, *end* it. The true-secret Unmaking: climb only to kill the Primordials and stop the Succession forever.
*Grid-alignment:* slides Free Wild (Chaos/Pure — liberation) to Devourer (Chaos/Corrupt — annihilation) depending on *your* heart; the Unbound is the one faction that will take you at either Chaos pole.

Nael is not a fanatic, which is what makes her terrifying. The zealots of the other factions burn hot; Nael is *cold* and *kind* and *right* in a way that's harder to argue with than any sermon. She learned the Succession truth — that every god was a mortal who climbed this ladder, that the thrones are a machine for grinding hope into tyranny forever — and where you might rage, she simply *concluded*: the only mercy is to stop the cycle. She doesn't hate the gods; she pities them, the way you'd pity a trap that's caught itself. She'll help you ascend if liberation is in you (Free Wild) or help you devour if ruin is (Devourer) — she's not particular about your color, only your *willingness to end things*. The Unbound's horror is that it might be the *good* ending, and that you'll never be sure. Nael smiles when she says the cycle stops with you. It's the saddest, kindest smile in the world, and it means the end of everything, including her.

## 9. The Deep Choir — *"the drowned remember everything you'd rather forget"*

**Region · God:** The Tideless · Poseidon · **Grid:** Balanced / Corrupt (the Plaguelord pole)
**Rival:** the Concord. **They offer:** leviathan creatures, Ouranos/Thanatos, deep secrets.

### Leader — **The Drowned Cantor, Vourl Who-Came-Back-Wrong**
*Title:* Cantor of the Deep Choir; Throat of the Tideless; the Listener at the Bottom.
*Voice:* layered, tidal, more-than-one; speaks as though translating something from very far down and not always getting it right on purpose.
*Goal:* eldritch communion — the drowned dead and the deep gods *remember*, and the Choir's worship is to *listen*, to learn what the surface buried, and to bring a little of the bottom up.
*Grid-alignment:* Plaguelord (Balanced/Corrupt) — courting Vourl pushes you Corrupt while keeping you off the Order/Chaos extremes; the Choir is balance gone *deep and wrong*.

Vourl drowned, and something down there found the body interesting, and what came back up is *mostly* Vourl plus a great deal of opinions that aren't. He — they — it is not malevolent; the Deep Choir doesn't *want* anything as small as harm. It wants to *remember*, and to be remembered, and the horror is in the scale of what's down there listening back. Vourl speaks in the plural because the drowned share a throat now, and sometimes a dead sailor's grief surfaces mid-sentence, and Vourl lets it, because silencing the dead is the one sin the Choir won't commit. The faction's grim joke is bureaucratic-eldritch: communion with vast drowned intelligences, conducted with the patience of a very old, very damp filing system. Vourl is gentle, and patient, and when it says *the deep remembers you* it is not a threat — it is the most permanent form of love the abyss has, and you cannot give it back.

- **Sworn:** *"You commune now. You've heard the low note and kept your shape, mostly. The drowned trust you with the small secrets. Walk the tide-vaults; mind the ones that ask you to stay. They ask everyone. They're patient."*
- **Champion:** *"The deepest tides open. Poseidon's drowned children are down there — leviathans that remember being gods, and the things that ate the parts that fell off. They'll consider you now. That's an honor. It's also the danger. They're the same thing, down here."*

### Gated quest hooks
