# Mutants_Game — Voice & Microcopy Library

**Status:** v0.1 · in-canon content build · **Last updated:** 2026-06-27
**Owner register:** funny-grim, mature-occult. Dread is bedrock; humor is the relief valve — gallows, deadpan, dry, self-deprecating, with *rare* unsettling 4th-wall cracks. **Litmus:** if a line makes you laugh and then slightly regret laughing, it's on-tone.

> **How to use this file.** A broad, reusable bank of in-voice strings for UI, toasts, barks, and flavor. Lines are grouped by category; most categories offer several variants so the game can roll one at random and stay fresh. Pull these into the string tables; don't paraphrase them into chipper-app voice. Where a line names a system (entropy, the Graveyard, "seal the rite," a force, a faction), that name is **canon** — keep it.
>
> **Voice rules honored throughout:** mature occult dread first, wit second; no real-world IP, slang, or memes; cruelty with a wink, never edgelord-for-shock; death/corruption/sacrifice stay *weighty* — the humor makes them land harder, never undercuts them. Colorblind-safe: no copy relies on a color word alone.
>
> **Token conventions:** `{creature}`, `{rival}`, `{faction}`, `{region}`, `{god}`, `{ally}`, `{n}`, `{part}`, `{save}` are runtime fills.

**Counts:** 300+ lines across 11 sections.

## 4. The Lab

The bench that breathes. The geneticist's showpiece — tactile, alive, consequential, *fun to fail at*. "Deep engine, simple surface": the cost ledger is legible; the dread is in the wording. Ops are **Mutate · Fuse · Build · Mod · Sacrifice**; method runs **precise ↔ wild**; the commit is "seal the rite."

### 4.1 Operation preview
- Place what you'll spend. The table is listening.
- The apparatus is ready. It is rarely *eager*; today it seems eager.
- Inputs accepted. The silhouette is already trying to become something.
- Two creatures in. One comes out. Decide who you'd miss.
- Drag the method toward wild and watch the rig start to argue with you.
- The ledger updates as you push. Read it. It's the only honest thing in here.

### 4.2 The commit — "seal the rite"

- Pull the lever. Whatever answers, answers in full.
- Sign here, in something that isn't ink.

### 4.3 The birth / reveal
- It's done. Something opened its eyes that did not have them an hour ago.
- The light settles. A new name resolves out of the smoke. Say it gently.
- Born. Whole. Yours. The bond clicks into place like a lock with no key.
- It worked. *It worked.* Stand there a moment and let that be enough.
- Out of the parts and the prayer: a thing that is wholly new and entirely your fault.
- The reveal: better than you hoped, stranger than you planned. Welcome it.

### 4.4 A botched splice

### 4.5 Taboo warning

- Opposed forces. An abomination. A bounty on your head the moment it draws breath. Seal it?
- This grafts a god's organ onto mortal meat. Gods notice their missing parts. Continue?

- You are about to make something the factions will burn cities to unmake. Worth it?

## 8. Rare 4th-Wall Lines

**Use sparingly.** A small, special set — unsettling-funny, not a running gag. The rule: a little *wrong*, never a quip. Gate these behind rare triggers (a specific signpost, an over-talked NPC, the ghost-in-the-code cameo, a save-name read aloud). If it ever feels like a recurring joke, it's overused — pull it.

- A signpost, hand-painted: "{save}. We KNOW it's you. We've always known."
- *(over-talked NPC)* "You've spoken to me six times. I'm out of lines. Frankly, so are you. We're both just here now, aren't we."
- *(the ghost-in-the-code — an NPC that shouldn't exist)* "I'm not in the file. Don't tell them I'm in the file."
- *(an NPC who suspects the truth, badly)* "Do you ever feel *watched*? Not by gods. By something with… *notes*?"
- *(after a reload following a death)* "You went away. Things happened without you. We agreed not to mention them. Don't ask."
- *(a creature, staring out of the screen, once, never again)* "…you're the one who decides, aren't you. Huh."
- *(deep corruption, an idle line)* "There's someone behind your eyes who isn't paying for any of this. I wonder if they sleep well."
- *(a fortune-teller, breaking)* "Your future? I can see your *saves*, child. There are so few of them. You're so careful. Why are you so careful?"
- *(very rare, on quitting late at night)* "Go on. Close the book. We'll be right here, in the dark, exactly as you left us. We don't mind the dark."
