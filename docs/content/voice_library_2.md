# Mutants_Game — Voice & Microcopy Library, Volume II

**Status:** v0.1 · in-canon content build · **Last updated:** 2026-06-28
**Owner register:** funny-grim, mature-occult. Dread is bedrock; humor is the relief valve — gallows, deadpan, dry, self-deprecating, with *rare* unsettling 4th-wall cracks. **Litmus:** if a line makes you laugh and then slightly regret laughing, it's on-tone.

> **What this volume is.** A *second* bank, written to complement `voice_library.md` (Volume I) — **not** to repeat it. Where Vol. I painted broad strokes (one UI voice, one battle voice, a handful of ambient registers), Vol. II goes **per-region**, **per-faction**, and deeper into the systems the first pass only sampled: shop/barter, breeding & husbandry, the five Lab ops by name, the six statuses inflicted/suffered/cured, weather & entropy-tide, onboarding, death/victory/defeat, rivalry & competition, and a small new set of 4th-wall cracks. ## 1. Per-Region Ambient

A distinct register for each of the eleven regions — the *land's* voice as much as the locals'. Vol. I gave a few hub/faction barks; this is the full set, one tonal key per region. Fire as ambient overlay text, zone-entry stings, or idle environmental murmur.

### 1.1 Threshold *(the hub — weary, wry, neutral mixing-pot)*
- The gate-bells count everyone in and no one out. They're honest about which number is bigger.
- Nine embassies, one truce, zero illusions. Welcome to the last city that still ages on schedule.
- Somewhere a Standstill bell is about to ring. Somewhere it always is. Try not to ascend in the bakery.
- "First time? The betting pool's already open on you. No, you can't see the odds. Yes, they're unkind."
- The cobbles here were never anyone's god. That's why they hold still. Enjoy the novelty.

### 1.2 The Verdant Glut *(Eros — warm, doomed, fecund-to-menacing)*
- Everything here is either healing or being eaten, and the Glut sees no daylight between the two.
- The fruit is ripe. The fruit is *always* ripe. That should comfort you less than it does.
- Stand still long enough and the green will tend to you. Tenderly. Permanently. Keep walking.
- "Mind the path, love — the path is the part that hasn't decided to be a mouth yet."
- A goddess lay down here and never got up, and the soil took it as encouragement.

### 1.3 The Mournmarch *(Thanatos — grey, courtly, patient subtraction)*
- The grass is the color of a held breath. It's been holding it since the Underlord went down.
- Nothing is gone here. Everything is merely *owed*, and the Court keeps excellent records.
- The rivers run slow because even the water has lost the will. You'll understand by dusk.
- "Don't grieve so loudly, dear. Grief is just power you haven't filed yet. Lower your voice and your overhead."
- A headstone here has your measurements. The Court took them when you weren't looking. They always look.

### 1.4 Forgefell *(Cosmos+Gaia — industrial, rigid, haunted by purpose)*
- The foundries never stopped. Nobody told them the god died. Nobody tells machines anything kind.
- Ten thousand automatons doing a job that ended an age ago, beautifully, forever. Mind the loop.
- Order, here, is a thing you can stub your toe on. The geometry has opinions about where you stand.
- "Everything in Forgefell was built to do something. Most of it is still doing it. Badly. Don't be most of it."
- The slag froze mid-pour when the maker fell. It's thawing. It keeps its own schedule, and it's coming due.

### 1.5 The Storm Vault *(Ouranos — high, vertiginous, motion-as-law)*
- To stand still here is to fall. The Vault has no patience for the grounded, and less for the slow.
- The islands drift, the storms reroute, the contracts mature. Only the House Edge stays put.
- Altitude is power and speed is freedom and the long way down is always, always available.
- "High Table's overhead, friend. Everything's overhead here. That's the whole pitch — pay up, climb up."
- A tavern drifted past a century ago with its patrons mid-drink. They wave. They made it work. They can't land.

### 1.6 The Sunder *(Chaos — anti-coherent, unwell, gleeful)*
- Up is a suggestion. Distance is negotiable. The map is a lovely drawing of a place that isn't here.
- You walked north for an hour and arrived behind yourself. Don't make it weird. One of you is real.
- The rules took the age off. The locals are filling in. They are doing a *terrible*, wonderful job.
- "Stay too long in one spot and you'll end up somewhere else — smaller, on fire, and oddly fine about it!"
- A door stands locked in an empty field. No wall, no house. Knocking does *something*. Nobody who knocked has said.

### 1.7 Titanfall *(Gaia — heavy, ancient, slow to anger)*
- The mountains here are corpses, and at least one of them is still, faintly, breathing. Tread soft.
- Mordathun is under your feet — the Old Weight, the Fallen Hill. Don't stamp. He remembers stamping.
- Things here are old, slow, and patient, and when they finally move, the ground does it with them.
- "You're in a hurry. The mountain was in a hurry once. Now it's a mountain. Sit. We've got time. We *are* time."
- The clans measure a life in generations and find your whole ascension faintly, fondly loud.

### 1.8 The Tideless *(Ouranos+Gaia — drowned, hushed, eldritch-patient)*
- The sea stopped here. It settled. The silence is so total that sound carries forever, and so does listening.
- Everything real sank a long time ago. The surface is just the part that hasn't gone down yet.
- The deep has had eons to learn your language from the drowned. It mostly pronounces your name right.
- "Come down a little. The first fathom's painless. We'll be honest about the rest once you can't leave."
- A leviathan's eye, the size of a door, opens, considers you, and decides you are not, today, interesting. Exhale.

### 1.9 The Astral Tier *(Cosmos — bright, airless, perfect, terrifying)*
- The light never changes. No day, no night, no surprise. Nothing unexpected has happened here in four hundred years.
- Every name is recorded, every flower is regulation height, and the one that grew crooked is being corrected. All day.
- Order is not cruel. Order is *thorough*. Please have your soul ready for inspection at the next seal.
- "A wild thing is just an unsigned contract. Hold still. This won't hurt. That is, precisely, the point of it."
- The most beautiful region in the world, and the most airless. You'll miss weather. They've never had any.

### 1.10 The Maw Beneath *(deep corruption — formless, indifferent, gallows-bleak)*
- Everything falls. That's the Maw's only law, and it is very, very patient about it.
- The Rot doesn't hate you. It doesn't know the word. It would eat a god and a turnip identically.
- The deeper you go, the less anything keeps its edges — including, eventually, the thought of *you*.
- "Found a god's finger down here last week. Still twitching. Sold it on. Lovely transaction. Want a thumb?"
- Down here a single Concord seal holds against an ocean of Rot. Futile. Magnificent. The seal does not care.

### 1.11 The Hollow Atelier *(player-authored — quiet, accountable, yours alone)*
- A place that did not exist until you made it. It is unfinished because *you* are. Keep building.
- The floors resolve only where you've earned ground. The rest is blank potential, waiting for a reason.
- The walls wear your portfolio — crystal where you built order, stone where you built weight, *wrong* where you didn't ask first.
- "Your Lab grew a geography. I've mopped botches. I've never mopped a *horizon*. New record. Hold the screaming; it has acoustics now."
- Every creature you scrapped left a mark here. The Atelier remembers the drafts. It's the most honest mirror in the world.

## 2. Per-Faction Voice

Each of the Nine in their own register, beyond the leader dialogue in `factions_npcs.md`. Three beats per faction: a **greeting** (generic standing-neutral hail), a **standing-change** pair (regard rises / regard falls), and a **refusal** (when you ask for something your standing or grid won't buy). Fire by faction id. Keep the creed audible: each faction is *right* about something, and wrong in a way they've made peace with.

### 2.1 The Concord *(Order/Pure — "order is the only mercy that scales")*
- *Greeting:* "State your business in full, and in order. The Concord processes intent; it does not guess at it."
- *Standing up:* "Your file improves. You did the lawful thing when the wild thing was faster. We have noted it, dated it, and — this is the warm part — *believed* it."
- *Standing down:* "An irregularity. We had you nearly regularized, and you went and did *that*. The file reopens. The file is so tired of reopening."
- *Refusal:* "Denied. Not unkindly — *consistently*. There is a form for what you want. You have not earned the form for the form."

### 2.2 The Iron Guild *(Order/Tainted — "a god that breaks was built wrong")*
- *Greeting:* "You stroke them and hope. We *build* them. Come back when you've stopped apologizing to your tools."
- *Standing up:* "Clean weld on that last one. Self-taught, by the scarring. The Guild's got a bench with your name half-stamped. We'll finish the stamp when you finish flinching."
- *Standing down:* "You went *sentimental* on the bench. Sentiment's the most expensive material there is, and you spent it on *that*. Scrap thinks better of itself."
- *Refusal:* "Not for you. Not yet. That recipe's above your tolerance — you'd build something that hunts us, and then I'd have to *service* it, and I hate doing returns."

### 2.3 The Pale Court *(Order/Corrupt — "death is just power no one's redeemed yet")*
- *Greeting:* "Welcome, welcome. Mind the ledger — everything in this room has a lien on it, including, increasingly, the air."

- *Refusal:* "Regrettably, no. That deposit is *old* — a god's own bloodline, held against a withdrawal no one's dared make. You are not, yet, daring enough. The Court can wait. The Court invented waiting."

### 2.4 The High Table *(Balanced — "there are no gods, only contracts that haven't lapsed")*

## 3. Shop & Barter

Merchant patter across the loop: buying, selling, can't-afford, the haggle, the black-market disclaimer, and the "blessed"/cursed-goods sales pitch. Currency glyphs stay canon — **₯** Drachma, **✶** Essence, **◈** Ichor. The vendor voice is transactional with a shadow: every sale is a small confession, and the merchant has stopped pretending otherwise.

### 3.1 Buying — confirm / on purchase
- "Sold. It's yours now, and so is whatever it does at night. No refunds on consequences."
- "{n}₯, and not a coin haggled. Take it before I remember what it's actually worth."
- "A fine choice. Or a terrible one. I've stopped being able to tell, and frankly so have my customers."
- "Wrapped and yours. Use it well, use it badly — the coin spends the same either way."
- "There. One transaction, clean as this trade gets, which is to say not very."

### 3.2 Selling — player sells to vendor
- "I'll give you {n}₯ for it. It's not what it's worth. Nothing here is paid what it's worth, least of all me."
- "Parts, is it? I'll take them off your hands. The drawer's always hungry, and the drawer always pays."
- "This came off something, didn't it. Don't tell me. The less I know, the more I'll offer. That's the whole economy down here."
- "Sold to me. I'll find it a worse home than yours, for a small markup. That's the trade."
- "{n}✶ for the lot. Soul-fuel's soul-fuel; I don't ask whose it was, you don't tell me, we both sleep poorly."

### 3.3 Can't afford
- "Come back with coin, or come back with something to trade that isn't *that look*. Two of those spend here. The look isn't one."
- "Short by {n}₯. The cosmos doesn't extend credit, and neither, sadly, do I — I tried it once. He's a headstone now."
- "Lovely taste. Empty purse. The two so often travel together. Off you go; the item will wait. It's patient. It's *merchandise*."

## 7. Weather, Entropy-Tide & Day-Night

Ambient overlay for the overworld's force-climate, the entropy-tide cycle (the world's pulse that opens/seals routes and ramps instability), and the turn of day into night. Fire as weather stings, tide announcements, or dusk/dawn idle text. The world is a dying thing with a heartbeat; let the ambient remind the player it's *watching*.

### 7.1 Force-weather (climate by pole)
- A Gaia-pressure front rolls in — the air goes *heavy*, the ground attentive. Bulk-blooded things wake. The rest of you should sit down.
- An Ouranos squall tears through. Everything light moves twice; everything slow moves once and regrets it.
- A Cosmos calm settles over the land — too even, too quiet, the weather of a place that has filed away its surprises.
- A Chaos-cell forms, and the sky forgets which way down is. Briefly. Enjoyably. Catastrophically.
- An Eros bloom-front passes through and the dead grass tries, hopefully, to grow. Some of it succeeds. Some of it shouldn't have.
- A Thanatos chill comes down like a held breath let out slow. Nothing dies that wasn't going to. It just dies *sooner*, and tidier.

### 7.2 Entropy-tide
- The entropy-tide is rising. The world's instability climbs with it — push hard now and the bench will push back harder.
- Low tide. The entropy settles, the routes that were sealed grind open, and the patient get their hour. Spend it.
- High entropy-tide — the slag thaws, the warps deepen, and everything volatile gets *more so*. The Sunder is delighted. You should be careful.
- The tide turns. Somewhere a tide-locked door opens; somewhere another seals behind it. The world breathes. You're inside the lung.
- Entropy crests. Every meter that can climb is climbing. This is when legends get made and creatures get unmade, often in the same heartbeat.

### 7.3 Day → night / dusk
- The light goes. In a graveyard of gods, the dark is just the part where you can hear them better.
- Dusk. The lamplighter starts his rounds — keeps the dark *organized*. Won't stop a god. Stops the smaller things. The smaller things are most of what gets you.
- Night falls over {region}. The day-beasts bed down; the other kind wake and go looking. Mind which kind you are.
- Last light. Whatever you were going to finish today, the day has stopped waiting for you to finish it.

### 7.4 Night → day / dawn
- Dawn, such as it is. The world survived another rotation, which it manages mostly out of habit now.
- First light finds you still here, still climbing. The cosmos files this under *persistent*. It's not a compliment. It's not *not* one.
- The dark lifts off {region} like a debt deferred, not paid. Morning's just the interest break. Use it.
- A new day. The Graveyard didn't grow overnight, which is either your doing or your luck. Don't ask which. Don't make it answer.

### 7.5 Climate shift / weather warning

## 9. Death, Permadeath, Victory & Defeat

The weighty beats — written *heavy*, never quippy. Death is mournful and load-bearing; victory always carries a shadow; defeat is a teacher, not a punchline. Complements Vol. I's short battle-frame set with longer, more ceremonial variants for the moments that should *land*. The humor here is the gallows kind: it makes the grief land harder, never lighter.

### 9.1 Permadeath — a creature, for good
- "{creature} is gone. Not down — *gone*. The bond doesn't fade; it *stops*, mid-note, the way a song does when the singer doesn't. A headstone waits in the Graveyard. Go when you're ready. You won't be."
- "It fell, and it stayed fallen, and some patient part of the world came to collect what it always meant to. You can rebuild what {creature} *was*. You will never again build who it *was*. Hold the difference. It's the only thing you get to keep."
- "{creature} has died. The Pale Court will offer to fix that. They always offer. Read the terms — read *all* the terms — and remember that the cleanest grief is the one you simply *carry*. One name leaves the ledger. The Graveyard keeps the rest, and the Graveyard never forgets a name. It's the only one of you that won't."
- "Gone. The portrait it was becoming — the one your champion carries into someone else's run — closes here, unfinished. Every act of care you spent on it was real. It was real *because* it ends. That's the whole grim bargain. You signed it the day you held out your hand."

### 9.2 Permadeath — context / the Graveyard
- "Another headstone. The Graveyard district has opinions now; some of the stones do, too. Walk it anyway. The dead you made deserve a visitor, and you're the only one they'll get."
- "Goodwife Penne logs the ones you lose. Name, force, how. Someone decent should do it, so she does. She'll ask which one this time, gently, the way you ask after a wound you already know is mortal."
- "The storage made room the way it always does — the dead make room. You felt the click of it. Try not to need the room again so soon. You will. But try."

### 9.3 Victory — with its shadow
- "They're down. You're up. The math, this time, was *kind* — and the Graveyard goes hungry today, which only means it'll be hungrier tomorrow. Collect your fallen. Count your own. Don't look too pleased; something always notices when you do."
- "Won. The circle clears, the dust settles, and you're the team that walked out whole. Savor it for exactly as long as it takes the next thing to find you. That's about a breath. Use it well."
- "Victory. You beat them honestly, or close enough to honestly that the difference is yours to keep. The world files the win, adjusts your notoriety up, and quietly sharpens the next rival to match. Winning is how you summon worse. Congratulations. Truly. Mind the worse."
- "Done, and done well. The thing about climbing a graveyard is that every win takes you *higher into it* — closer to the big dead, the patient ones, the gods who used to lose for a living. You're getting good at this. That should frighten you more than losing ever did."

### 9.4 Defeat — the teacher
- "Your squad is broken. You are not dead — only *taught*. Gather what's left of them; some of it still walks, some of it fits in a jar, all of it is still yours. The world notes the loss, files it, and waits. It's patient. It is *so* patient. It can out-wait anything but you, if you let it."
- "You lost. There's no shine on it and I won't pretend there is. But a loss in this world is rarely the *end* of you — it's the tuition. Pay attention to what it cost and you'll have bought something. Ignore it and you'll just buy it again, dearer."
- "Defeat. The rival walks off wearing the look — the one *after* the worst part — and you're left counting. Counting's good. Counting is how the taught become the dangerous. Count, rebuild, and come back as the thing *they* file a loss against."
- "Down, but the bond held — the body just needs a minute the world may not give. Get them somewhere safe before the somewhere stops being safe. You're a loss today. 'Today' is the operative word, and it's the only word in this whole place that's ever on your side."

### 9.5 The big losses / run-ending weight *(use rarely, for high stakes)*
- "That one mattered. You'll feel the shape of its absence for a long while — in the empty slot, the unfilled lineage, the order you'll go to give and then won't. The world doesn't pause for it. The world never pauses. But you can. For a moment. Go on."
- "A run can end. They do. If this is the end of yours, know that the climb was always the point and the fall was always the price, and that somewhere your champion is snapshotted into a throne, waiting to be someone else's worst morning. You don't vanish. You become a *boss*. It's the closest thing to mercy the Succession sells."

### 9.6 Creature downed (KO — not dead) *(extended)*
- "{creature} drops — not gone, just *unfit to continue*. Filed for revival, breathing, furious, still yours. Get it standing before the fight decides 'down' should mean 'down for good.'"
- "{creature} taps out. The bond holds; the body just needs a minute the battlefield rarely grants. Cover the gap. A knockout's only temporary if you survive long enough to make it so."
- "Down goes {creature}, eyes still tracking you from the dirt, already plotting the rematch. Knocked out, not lost. There's a whole gulf between those two words, and it's your job to keep them apart."

### 9.7 Death — region-flavored *(fire by location)*
- *(Mournmarch):* "{creature} falls in the grey country, where the land's so saturated with endings it forgot how to begin — fitting ground for one. The Pale Court already has the paperwork. Of course they do. They had it before you arrived."

### 9.8 Battle start *(extended — beyond Vol. I)*
