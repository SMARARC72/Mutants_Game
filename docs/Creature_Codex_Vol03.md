# MUTANTS_GAME — Creature Codex · Volume 3

**Scope:** S2-01–S2-30 (the first thirty Set-2 "teen" forms — the cute→grim transition tier). 30 entries.
**Status:** Vol3 complete — S2-01…S2-30 done. *(Mid-stage tier; matches the Vol1 gold standard for format/voice.)*
**Method:** funny-grim · stats from live `stat_engine.py` (copied verbatim from dossier Vol03) · art read from `montage_set2_A.png` (S2-1…S2-30), row-cropped and upscaled to confirm force/role/body.

> **Coordination flags for the systems session (do not silently fix — your call):**
> 1. **All thirty are registry `stage=mid`, `tier=T2`, `line_registry=[none]`** — every line below is a *proposed* clade I named from force+role+art kinship. Baby (SB/T1) and apex (T3/Set-2B) anchors are **suggested in NOTES, never hard-assigned.** Ratify or override before they enter `line_registry`.
> 2. **Mono-force reads:** the dossier lists every Set-2 mid as `Force/-` (single pole, secondary blank). I kept the entries mono-force to match. Where the art hints at a faint second pole (e.g. S2-6's Eros-bright wings, S2-18's Ouranos crackle), I flag it in NOTES as a *candidate* secondary for the adult form — I did not invent a blend.
> 3. **Stat uniformity:** all thirty share the canonical T2 mid spread (one stat at 210, rest at 30; Vitality builds read HP 870, the rest HP 330). Copied verbatim. This is expected for a same-tier cohort, not an error.
> 4. **Likely apex bridges:** several mids clearly point at Vol1 adult lines or Set-2B apexes (Gravetusk, Tidecoil, Gloamcat, Emberwyrm, an Augur flight). Cross-volume stitch candidates are flagged per entry.
