40 painterly icons (forces, stats, ranks, class, acquisition, skill verbs, lab verbs, statuses)
generated for this project into the main-repo art library (`/assets/icons/**`, gitignored) and

premultiplied-alpha LANCZOS downscale 256 → 128 RGBA. First-party output — no third-party
license obligations. Regenerate by re-running the script against the library; never hand-edit.
