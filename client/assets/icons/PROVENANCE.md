# Icons — game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0, 🟡)

Curated SVGs for the 6 forces · 8 verbs · 6 statuses · 3 currencies · 5 gear slots, renamed to our
domain terms. **Attribution is required** and recorded per-icon in the repo-root `CREDITS.md`.
Source: https://game-icons.net · Phase 5 recolors/styles these to the design system.

**Wave 4:** the baked full-canvas backdrops (which rendered every icon as a black box) were
stripped by `tools/restyle_icons.py` (idempotent; re-run any time). Glyphs remain `#fff` for
tint-by-modulate on ink surfaces. The `painterly/` subfolder is a separate first-party set —
see its own PROVENANCE.md.
